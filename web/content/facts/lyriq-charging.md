## Adapters and other networks

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tesla Supercharger access, 2025 and 2026 | A GM-approved NACS adapter; without an adapter, a Supercharger fitted with the Tesla Magic Dock[^12][^14][^11] |
| Non-NACS home and public chargers, 2027 | A GM-approved adapter[^13][^11] |
| Adapters and the battery warranty, 2025 and 2026 | Damage to the battery from an adapter that General Motors does not sell or approve is excluded from the New Vehicle Limited Warranty[^15][^16][^10] |
| Vehicle-to-Home, 2026 | Standard V2H compatibility; supplying the house requires the GM Energy PowerShift charger and the GM Energy V2H Enablement Kit[^12] |
