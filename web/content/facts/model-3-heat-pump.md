# Tesla Model 3: Heat Pump by Build Date

## Heat pump by build date

Tesla draws the line at a manufacture date, not a model year.

| Build date | Heat pump |
|---|---|
| Built approximately July 2017 to October 2020 | None[^1] |
| Built after approximately October 2020 | Standard[^1] |

Tesla's own words: "Vehicles manufactured between approximately July 2017 and October 2020 do not have a heat pump." The word "approximately" is Tesla's.[^1]

- To check one specific car, touch Controls > Software > Additional Vehicle Information.[^2]

## Scope note

US model years 2017 through 2026, but the boundary is a manufacture date, approximately October 2020, so a car built near it cannot be resolved from its model year alone. No exact changeover date or VIN range is claimed. Rear-Wheel Drive, Long Range and Performance are not broken out, because Tesla's statement does not break them out.
