# Volkswagen ID.4 (model years 2021–2025, US market): Heat Pump by Year

## 2021–2025

No US-market Volkswagen ID.4 built for model years 2021 through 2025 has a heat pump, on any trim, drivetrain, or battery pack.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Est. At the ID.4's US launch, Volkswagen did not offer a heat pump for the US market. The ID.4 shipped with heated front seats and a heated steering wheel standard instead, and used an electric-resistance heater.[^5]

Est. A heat pump was offered as a paid option on Canada-bound ID.4 builds, and on European ID.4/ID.3 models. Volkswagen stopped offering it on Canada-bound builds in December 2022, citing a semiconductor shortage. That change did not affect the US, because the US-market ID.4 was never sold with a heat pump to begin with. Volkswagen's own statement on this reaches this sheet only through two news outlets that both quote the same VW spokesperson; no directly fetchable Volkswagen press release or newsroom statement was found for it.[^6]

## 2026

Not covered. No primary document for model year 2026 was opened for this sheet.
