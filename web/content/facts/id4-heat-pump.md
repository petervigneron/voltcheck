# Volkswagen ID.4: Heat Pump by Model Year

## Heat pump by model year

| Model year | Heat pump |
|---|---|
| 2021 | Est. None[^1] |
| 2022 | None, every trim, drivetrain and battery pack[^2] |
| 2023 | None, every trim, drivetrain and battery pack[^3] |
| 2024 | None, every trim, drivetrain and battery pack[^4] |
| 2025 | None, every trim, drivetrain and battery pack[^5] |

## What it heats with instead

| Equipment | At the ID.4's US launch |
|---|---|
| Cabin heater | Est. Electric resistance heater[^1] |
| Heated front seats | Est. Standard[^1] |
| Heated steering wheel | Est. Standard[^1] |

## Canada and Europe

- Est. A heat pump was a paid option on Canada-bound ID.4 builds and on European ID.4 and ID.3 models; Volkswagen stopped offering it on Canada-bound builds in December 2022, citing a semiconductor shortage.[^6]

Model years 2021 through 2025, US market; model year 2026 is not checked and not claimed. The 2021 row and the launch-equipment rows rest on secondary reporting dated to launch rather than a Volkswagen document, and are marked Est.; 2022 through 2025 come from Volkswagen's own US retail order guides.
