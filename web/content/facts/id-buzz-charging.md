# Volkswagen ID. Buzz: Charging

## DC fast charging

| Model year | Peak DC rate | 10% to 80% |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 200 kW, standard on all five trims[^1] | About 26 min[^2] |

## Home charging (Level 2)

| Model year | Onboard AC charger | Charging cable |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 11 kW, standard on all five trims[^1] | Not included; a 2-in-1 240V and 120V cable is a separate accessory[^1] |

## Connector and adapters

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Port type | Not stated outright in any Volkswagen document opened for this sheet; Volkswagen's DC adapter is described as North American Charging System (NACS) to Combined Charging System (CCS) port, in a release addressed to ID.4 and ID. Buzz owners[^3] |
| Tesla Supercharger access | Opened November 18, 2025; requires a Volkswagen-approved NACS adapter[^3] |
| Adapter MSRP | $200, at Volkswagen dealers and parts.vw.com[^3] |
| Rebate | $100 for original owners of model year 2025 vans, claimed within 90 days of the adapter purchase date and no later than July 15, 2026[^3] |
| Model year 2026 | An adapter comes standard with every 2026 Volkswagen EV[^3] |

- The adapter is for DC fast charging only — not designed for Level 1 or Level 2 AC equipment, including home chargers and Tesla Destination Chargers.[^3]
- Volkswagen strongly recommends a software update before using the adapter on certain 2024 and 2025 vehicles.[^3]

## Preconditioning

| Model year | Automatic | Manual | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Starts on the way to the next quick-charging station when the native navigation's Electric Vehicle Route Planner guidance is active[^2] | Charging menu in the Infotainment system, without active route guidance[^2] | Battery heated to optimum temperature before the stop; charging time reduced by several minutes, particularly in winter[^2] |

## Battery packs

| Model year | Pack | Trims |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 91 kWh gross lithium-ion with Performance Drive Unit, 13 cell modules[^1][^2] | Standard on all five — Pro S, Pro S Plus RWD, Pro S Plus 4Motion, 1st Edition RWD, 1st Edition 4Motion[^1] |

## Included charging plan

| Item | Terms |
|---|---|
| What is included | 500 kWh of Electrify America charging plus 36 months of Pass+ membership, which Volkswagen says saves about 25% against Guest and Pass base rates[^2] |
| Who gets it | New model year 2025 ID. Buzz purchased from an authorized U.S.-based Volkswagen dealer[^2] |
| Starts | Upon vehicle purchase[^2] |
| Transferable | No[^2] |
| Commercial use | Not available for ride-hailing or ridesharing[^2] |
| Plug&Charge | Original purchaser or owner only, for the duration of the included plan; deactivates when the 3-year plan expires[^2] |
| Plug&Charge networks | Electrify America DC fast and Level 2 public stations only[^2] |

Volkswagen on what a second owner gets: "Not valid for subsequent owners."[^2]
