# Mercedes-Benz GLE plug-in hybrid: Battery Warranty

## High-voltage battery limited warranty, all states

| Model year | Plug-in GLE | Term |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | GLE 450e 4MATIC SUV[^1][^4] | 6 years or 62,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^1] |
| 2025 | GLE 450e 4MATIC SUV[^2][^5] | 6 years or 62,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^2] |
| 2026 | GLE 450e 4MATIC SUV[^3][^6] | 6 years or 62,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^3] |

- The battery coverage supplements the New Vehicle Limited Warranty of 48 months or 50,000 miles, whichever occurs first, and covers defects arising after that warranty expires.[^1][^2][^3]

## SULEV plug-ins registered in California and listed states

| Model year | High-voltage battery, SULEV cars | States |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^1] | CA, CO, CT, MA, MD, ME, NJ, NY, OR, RI and VT[^1] |
| 2025 | 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^2] | CA, CO, CT, MA, MD, ME, MN, NJ, NV, NY, OR, RI, VA, VT and WA, in the coverage chart and the California Extended section (printed p. 100)[^2] |
| 2026 | 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^3] | CA, CO, CT, MA, MD, ME, MN, NJ, NV, NY, OR, RI, VA, VT and WA, in the coverage chart and the California Extended section (printed p. 100)[^3] |

- The 2025 and 2026 booklets' emission parts-list note, which also gives the High Voltage Battery 10 years or 150,000 miles, names 11 states: CA, CO, CT, MA, MD, ME, NJ, NY, OR, RI and VT. It leaves out MN, NV, VA and WA (2025 printed p. 57; 2026 printed p. 59).[^2][^3]
- The longer term is the California Extended High Voltage Battery Warranty. It covers new plug-in hybrids that meet the SULEV emission standard, first sold by an authorized Mercedes-Benz dealership and registered in one of the listed states.[^1][^2][^3]
- A car's SULEV status is shown on the Vehicle Emission Control Information (VECI) label in the engine compartment.[^1][^2][^3]

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | Repairs or replacements necessary to correct defects in material or workmanship to the high-voltage battery arising after the New Vehicle Limited Warranty expires[^1][^2][^3] |
| Capacity claims | A replacement battery will be in a condition appropriate to the age and mileage of the vehicle, sufficient to achieve or exceed the minimum battery capacity for the remainder of the original battery's warranty period[^1][^2][^3] |
| Repair standard | The method used to measure battery capacity, the choice to repair, replace or provide reconditioned or re-manufactured parts, and the condition of those parts are at the sole discretion of Mercedes-Benz[^1][^2][^3] |
| Transfers to a second owner | Yes. MBUSA warrants the high-voltage battery to the original and each subsequent owner[^1][^2][^3] |
| Clock starts | The same date as the New Vehicle Limited Warranty[^1][^2][^3] |
| That date, 2024 and 2025 | Delivery to the first retail purchaser, or entry into service as an authorized dealership demonstrator or an MBUSA, MBUSI or MBRDNA company vehicle, but no later than 18 months from the production date[^1][^2] |
| That date, 2026 | Except as otherwise required by applicable law, delivery to the first retail purchaser or lessee, or entry into service as an authorized dealership demonstrator or an MBUSA, MBUSI or MBRDNA company vehicle, but no later than 24 months from the production date[^3] |
| Owner's responsibility | Every required maintenance service must be accomplished to keep the battery coverage valid[^1][^2][^3] |
| Where it is valid | The same geographic restrictions as the New Vehicle Limited Warranty, including restrictions against its applicability outside the United States and Puerto Rico[^1][^2][^3] |

## What is not covered

In addition to the items the New Vehicle Limited Warranty excludes, the 2024 through 2026 booklets exclude damage or failures resulting from or caused by:

- Physically damaging or intentionally attempting to reduce the life of the high-voltage battery.[^1][^2][^3]
- Exposing the high-voltage battery to a direct flame.[^1][^2][^3]
- Immersing any portion of the high-voltage battery in water or fluids.[^1][^2][^3]
- Opening the high-voltage battery enclosure, or having it serviced by someone other than an authorized Mercedes-Benz Dealership.[^1][^2][^3]
- Neglecting to follow correct charging procedures as recommended in the Operator's Manual.[^1][^2][^3]
- Using incompatible charging devices.[^1][^2][^3]
- Failure to repair an existing problem or otherwise properly maintain the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3]
- Using the vehicle as a power source for alternative functions other than factory installed equipment.[^1][^2][^3]

The same booklets also exclude the costs of repairing damage or conditions caused by:

- Accident, collision, or object striking the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3]
- Towing of the vehicle (Flatbed recommended).[^1][^2][^3]
- Abuse or negligence.[^1][^2][^3]
- Failure to operate the vehicle in accordance with the Operator's Manual.[^1][^2][^3]
- Misuse, such as towing, driving over curbs, overloading, using the vehicle as a power source, or for any other purpose for which the vehicle is not designed.[^1][^2][^3]
- Use of replacement parts other than Genuine Mercedes-Benz parts.[^1][^2][^3]
- Improper repair or maintenance.[^1][^2][^3]
- Fire, explosion, earthquake, windstorm, lightning, hail, flood.[^1][^2][^3]
- Normal wear and tear.[^1][^2][^3]
- Failure to repair an existing problem or otherwise properly maintain the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3]

- Loss of capacity due to or resulting from gradual capacity loss is not covered beyond the terms and limits of the battery warranty.[^1][^2][^3]
- The battery warranty provides no coverage of any kind if the owner, insurer, financing institution or leasing company determined the vehicle to be a "total loss," "write off," or equivalent, or for any parts repaired or replaced under an insurance claim which are not required under the vehicle or battery warranty coverage.[^1][^2][^3]
