# Volkswagen ID. Buzz: Heat Pump by Model Year

## Heat pump by model year

| Model year | Trims | Heat pump |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Pro S, Pro S Plus, Pro S Plus 4MOTION, 1st Edition, 1st Edition 4MOTION | None[^1][^2] |

## What it heats with instead

| Equipment | Availability |
|---|---|
| Electric resistance heater, part of the Climatronic system | Standard — it also preconditions the battery for DC fast charging[^2] |
| Three-zone Climatronic automatic climate control | Standard[^2] |
| Heated steering wheel | Standard[^2] |
| Heated windshield washer nozzles | Standard[^2] |
| Heated and actively ventilated front seats | Standard, all five trims[^1] |
| Heated 2nd-row outboard seats with separate controls | Standard, all five trims[^1] |
| Heated windshield | Pro S Plus 4MOTION only[^1] |

Volkswagen's own words: "the ID. Buzz also has an electric resistance heater as part of the Climatronic system".[^2]

## Scope note

Model year 2025, US market, is the only model year covered; it is the ID. Buzz's first US model year. Model year 2026 is not checked and not claimed either way — no MY2026 US order guide or press kit was located. European and other non-US ID. Buzz builds are out of scope.
