# Volkswagen ID. Buzz (2025 model year, US market): Heat Pump

## 2025

No US-market 2025 Volkswagen ID. Buzz has a heat pump, on any of the five trims Volkswagen sells: Pro S, Pro S Plus (RWD), Pro S Plus 4MOTION, 1st Edition (RWD), and 1st Edition 4MOTION.[^1][^2]

Volkswagen names the heating hardware instead of leaving it unsaid. Volkswagen's own words: "Beyond those features, the ID. Buzz also has an electric resistance heater as part of the Climatronic system, which is designed to get to temperature faster than the traditional heaters from gas-powered vehicles, which can sometimes blow cold air until the engine warms up."[^2]

That same electric-resistance heater is what warms the battery before a fast charge. Volkswagen's own words: "This heater can also precondition the battery for DC fast charging—either automatically, when a charger is input as a destination in navigation, or manually triggered by the driver."[^2]

## What the ID. Buzz has instead

- Three-zone Climatronic automatic climate control, standard. Volkswagen's own words: "Standard three-zone Climatronic® automatic climate control keeps the cabin comfortable no matter the season, and all three rows have dedicated air vents."[^2]
- Heated steering wheel, standard. Heated windshield washer nozzles, standard. Volkswagen's own words: "When temperatures drop outside, the standard heated steering wheel keeps drivers cozy; heated windshield washer nozzles are standard and a heated windshield available on Pro S Plus 4Motion models, all helping to maximize efficiency."[^2]
- "Heated & actively ventilated front seats" and "Heated 2nd-row seats (outboard seating positions) w/ separate controls," both standard on all five trims. A heated windshield is standard on Pro S Plus 4MOTION and marked as not available on the other four trims.[^1]

## How this was checked

- The word "pump" does not appear anywhere in any of the four Volkswagen documents opened for this sheet: the MY2025 US retail order guide, the 2025 ID. Buzz press release, the 2025 ID. Buzz "At a Glance" sheet, or the 2025 ID. Buzz Tech Specs sheet.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- Silence alone is not the finding here. The order guide's climate rows are granular enough to break out heated washer nozzles from a heated windshield and to gate the heated windshield to a single trim, and Volkswagen's press release states positively what the heater is. A heat pump is not omitted from these documents; a different heating system is named in its place.[^1][^2]

## Scope note

Model year 2025, US market, is the only model year covered. It is the ID. Buzz's first US model year. No MY2026 US order guide and no MY2026 US press kit were located, so model year 2026 is neither checked nor claimed either way.

European and other non-US ID. Buzz builds are out of scope. No Volkswagen document stating a heat pump for any market's ID. Buzz was found either — the European newsroom's standard-and-optional-equipment page and data sheet for the ID. Buzz were both read, and neither uses the words "heat pump" — so this page makes no claim about what other markets get, in either direction.

The order guide was retrieved from volkswagenorderguides.com rather than from Volkswagen's own host. Its internal PDF title is Volkswagen's own filename, "VW MY25 Price and Order Guide for CW34-2024 - ID.Buzz 2024-08-22.pub," and a second copy fetched independently from a Volkswagen dealer's own asset store hashes identically, so both mirrors are the same file. The press release, At a Glance, and Tech Specs documents all came from media.vw.com, Volkswagen's own host.
