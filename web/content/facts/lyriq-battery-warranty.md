# Cadillac Lyriq: Battery Warranty

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The propulsion battery pack and its internal components, against defects in materials or workmanship[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Term | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Capacity floor | 75% of original value; the battery is replaced or repaired if capacity falls below it during the warranty period, as determined by a certified dealer[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Replacement battery | One appropriate for the age and mileage of the vehicle[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Repair parts | New or refurbished components, at Cadillac's discretion[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Where repairs are done | An authorized Cadillac dealership; Cadillac has a network of certified dealers trained to repair electric-vehicle battery packs[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is first delivered or put in use[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Cost to the owner | Parts, labor and towing at no charge[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Bumper-to-bumper coverage | 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^2][^3][^4] |

## Transfer to a later owner

| Model year | Answer |
|---|---|
| 2023 | Provided to the original and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period, and the battery coverage transfers at no cost within the 8 years or 100,000 miles term, with no deductibles[^1] |
| 2024 | Provided to the original and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period, and the battery coverage transfers at no cost within the 8 years or 100,000 miles term, with no deductibles[^2] |
| 2025 | Provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period[^3] |
| 2026 | Provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period[^4] |

## Towing to an EV Certified Cadillac dealer

| Model year | Answer |
|---|---|
| 2023 | Covered during the 8 years or 100,000 miles battery warranty period if the vehicle cannot be driven because of a warranted defect[^1] |
| 2024 | Covered during the 8 years or 100,000 miles battery warranty period if the vehicle cannot be driven because of a warranted defect[^2] |

## Using the pack for something other than driving

| Model year | Covered | Excluded |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Normal use for backup power during outages, reasonably selling energy back to the grid, and reasonable use as a mobile power source such as job-site power or vehicle-to-vehicle charging[^3] | Excessive non-propulsion use, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions; General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use it determines to be harmful to the battery[^3] |
| 2026 | Normal use for backup power during outages, reasonably selling energy back to the grid, and reasonable use as a mobile power source such as job-site power or vehicle-to-vehicle charging[^4] | Excessive non-propulsion use, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions; General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use it determines to be harmful to the battery[^4] |

## What is not covered

Model years 2023 through 2026:

- Alteration, modification or tampering with the vehicle after final assembly, including its body, chassis, driveline or software.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- Coverage where the odometer has been disconnected, its reading altered, or mileage cannot be determined.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- Vehicles currently or previously titled as salvaged, scrapped, junked or otherwise considered a total loss: the warranty is void.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- Damage from collision, fire, theft, freezing, vandalism, riot, explosion or objects striking the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- Damage from failure to follow the recommended maintenance schedule intervals.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

Model years 2025 and 2026:

- Damage from incompatible charging methods, or from a charging adapter that General Motors does not sell or approve.[^3][^4]
- Damage resulting from failure to install a Cadillac Over-the-Air software update within 45 days of it becoming available to the vehicle.[^3][^4]

## California, Massachusetts, New York, Oregon, Vermont and Washington

Model year 2026, vehicles under 10,000 lbs GVWR:

| Coverage | Term | What triggers it |
|---|---|---|
| High voltage battery | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^5][^6] | A defect in materials or workmanship that causes the battery state of health to deteriorate to less than 70%[^6] |
| Propulsion related part, long-term defects | 7 years or 70,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^5][^6] | A defect in a propulsion-related part the booklet marks with an asterisk, among them the Hybrid/Electric Vehicle Battery Pack, High Voltage Cables, Front Drive Unit, Rear Drive Unit, Drive Motor Power Inverter and Battery Charger[^6] |
| Propulsion related part, short-term defects | 3 years or 50,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^5][^6] | A defect in any propulsion-related part on the vehicle[^6] |

This coverage applies where both hold: the vehicle is certified for sale in California, per its emission control information label, and it is serviced by an authorized GM dealership in California or in a state that has adopted California's emission warranty requirements. Where either fails, the terms above do not apply and the coverage in the rest of this booklet does.[^6]

A 2026 Lyriq carries both floors: 75% under Battery Capacity Coverage everywhere, and 70% state of health under the terms above where they apply.[^4][^6]

## Scope note

US-market Cadillac Lyriq, model years 2023 through 2026, each read from the Cadillac electric-vehicle warranty booklet published for that model year. The Lyriq-V is a separate model and sits outside this scope, as does model year 2027. The booklet delivered with a specific car is the authority for that car.
