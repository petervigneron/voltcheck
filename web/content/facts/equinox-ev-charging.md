## Connector and adapters

| Model year | Charge port |
|---|---|
| 2024 | Combined Charging System 1 (CCS1)[^8] |
| 2025 | Combined Charging System 1 (CCS1)[^9] |
| 2026 | Combined Charging System 1[^10] |
| 2027 | North American Charging Standard (NACS), from the factory[^7][^11][^12] |

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tesla Supercharger access, CCS1 cars | A GM-approved NACS DC adapter, plus public charging set up in the myChevrolet app. Select generation 3 (V3) and beyond Superchargers are open to GM drivers[^13] |
| Adapter price and warranty | $225 MSRP, 12-month limited warranty from the date of purchase[^13] |
| Tesla Level 2 chargers | Not compatible. The NACS DC adapter is for Tesla Superchargers only[^13] |
| CCS stations, 2027 NACS-native cars | A NACS-to-CCS adapter[^12] |
| Level 1 and Level 2 charging, 2027 NACS-native cars | A J-1772 cable[^12] |
| Third-party adapters | Damage from an adapter GM did not sell, provide or approve is outside the vehicle warranty; GM discourages third-party adapters[^9][^13] |
| 2025 accessory adapter | Order code RYZ, NACS DC adapter for public use on NACS fast charging stations including Tesla Supercharger, IONNA, EVgo and others, for CCS native inlet vehicles[^5] |

## Scope note

US Equinox EV, model years 2024 through 2027. Chevrolet's charging figures for model year 2025 come from its 2025 specification guide and its owner's manual; the trim, order-code and package conditions above are that guide's, and a guide for one model year does not describe another. Where two Chevrolet documents give different figures for the same model year, both are shown, and the Level 2 table names the document each figure comes from.
