# Cadillac Escalade IQ and Escalade IQL: Battery Warranty

## Model years

| Model | Model years | Warranty booklet |
|---|---|---|
| Escalade IQ | 2025, 2026[^5][^6] | 2025: *2025 Cadillac Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information*[^1][^5]; 2026: *2026 Cadillac Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information*[^3][^5] |
| Escalade IQL | 2026[^5][^6] | *2026 Cadillac Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information*[^3][^5] |

## Coverage at a glance

Model years 2025 and 2026:

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The propulsion battery pack and its internal components[^1][^3] |
| Term | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^3] |
| Capacity floor | 75% of its original value; the battery is replaced or repaired if capacity falls below it during the warranty period, as determined by a certified dealer[^1][^3] |
| Replacement battery | One appropriate for the age and mileage of the vehicle[^1][^3] |
| Repair parts | New or refurbished components, at Cadillac's discretion, for a propulsion battery that needs service due to a defect in materials or workmanship[^1][^3] |
| Where repairs are done | An authorized Cadillac dealership[^1][^3] |
| Cost to the owner | Towing, parts and labor at no charge[^1][^3] |
| Transfers to a later owner | Yes. The warranty is provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period[^1][^3] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is first delivered or put in use[^1][^3] |
| Vehicles it applies to | Cadillac vehicles originally sold, registered, and normally operated in the United States[^1][^3] |
| Bumper-to-bumper coverage | 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^3] |

## Using the pack for something other than driving

Model years 2025 and 2026:

| Covered | Not covered |
|---|---|
| Normal use for backup power during outages, reasonably selling energy back to the electrical grid, and reasonable use as a mobile power source (job site power or vehicle to vehicle charging)[^1][^3] | Excessive use for non-propulsion purposes, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions; General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use that is determined to be harmful to the battery[^2][^4] |

## What is not covered

Model years 2025 and 2026. The booklets' exclusions include:

- Damage to the vehicle's battery from incompatible charging methods, or from an adapter that is not sold or approved by General Motors.[^2][^4]
- Damage resulting from failure to install Over-the-Air software updates; the owner is responsible for ensuring all Cadillac provided Over-the-Air software updates are installed within 45 days of software availability to the vehicle.[^2][^4]
- Alteration, modification, or tampering to the vehicle, including the body, chassis, driveline, software, or other components after final assembly.[^2][^4]
- Non-GM parts, control module calibrations, software modifications, and other alterations not issued through General Motors: they void the coverage for the components they damage or otherwise affect.[^2][^4]
- Damage from aftermarket vehicle propulsion enhancement products or modifications, which may not be covered.[^2][^4]
- Damage caused by collision, fire, theft, freezing, vandalism, riot, explosion, or objects striking the vehicle.[^2][^4]
- Water or fluid contamination.[^2][^4]
- Damage caused by failure to follow the recommended maintenance schedule intervals and/or failure to use or maintain proper fluids, lubricants, or refrigerants between maintenance intervals recommended in the Owner's Manual.[^2][^4]
- Vehicles where the odometer has been disconnected, its reading altered, or mileage cannot be determined.[^2][^4]
- Vehicles currently or previously titled as salvaged, scrapped, junked, or otherwise considered a total loss: the warranty is void.[^2][^4]
