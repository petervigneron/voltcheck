## What the EV System Warranty covers

| Model year | Components |
|---|---|
| 2019, 2020 | Electric Motor, Gear Drive Unit, Battery pack ("EV Battery"), Electric Power Control Unit (EPCU), On Board Charger (OBC), and all internal parts[^1][^2] |
| 2021 to 2026 | Electric Motor, High voltage Battery, Electric Power Control Unit (EPCU), On Board Charger (OBC)[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Repair standard | The original EV Battery is repaired and returned to the vehicle if possible; if necessary it is replaced with a new or remanufactured Lithium-Ion Polymer Battery. A repair or replacement may not return the battery to an "as new" 100% capacity[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |
| Transfers to a second owner | Yes. Any remaining portion of any warranty is fully transferable to subsequent owners except the 120 months/100,000 miles Power Train (Original Owner) warranty[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |
| Clock starts | Date of First Service: the earliest of delivery to the first retail purchaser, lease, or placement into service as a company vehicle such as a demonstrator, rental or fleet vehicle[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |
| Outside the EV System Warranty | Any other electrical components in the vehicle, such as (but not limited to) the traditional 12 volt car battery, alternator, or other starter components[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |

## What is not covered

Every manual from 2019 to 2026 excludes EV System and Lithium-Ion Polymer Battery damage or failure caused by:

- Ambient temperatures above 122F (50C) for over 24 hours, or storage below -22F (-30C) for over seven days.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Leaving the vehicle over 14 days where the battery reaches a zero or near zero state of charge.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Physically damaging the battery, intentionally attempting to reduce its life, exposing it to a direct flame, or immersing any portion of it in water or fluids.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Opening the battery enclosure, or having it serviced by someone other than a Kia certified EV technician.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Neglecting to follow correct charging procedures, or use of incompatible charging devices.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Incidental or consequential damage caused by the failure to repair an existing problem.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

The 2023 to 2026 manuals also exclude damage to the charge port or vehicle components caused by a public charger, a non-Kia supplied charge cord, or other third party electric vehicle service equipment (EVSE).[^5][^6][^7][^8]
