# Audi Q6 e-tron: Battery Warranty

## High-voltage battery warranty, all states

| Model year | Term |
|---|---|
| 2025[^4] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (February 2025 edition)[^1] |
| 2027[^4] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (February 2026 edition)[^2] |

## Federal Rechargeable Energy Storage System (RESS) warranty, 2027

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Term | 8 years or 80,000 miles from the in-service date, whichever occurs first[^3] |
| What is covered | Defects in manufacturer's material and workmanship on all components which have the primary purpose of high voltage system charging, high voltage system energy storage, and transmission of electrical power to move the vehicle[^3] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is delivered to either the original purchaser or the original lessee; or, if first placed in service as a demonstrator or company car, the date it is first placed in service[^3] |

## Coverage at a glance, 2025 and 2027

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The Audi e-tron high voltage battery and battery module[^1][^2] |
| Capacity coverage | Repair when the battery's SOH (State of Health) is below 70% compared to the condition at the time of delivery, within 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^1][^2] |
| Repair standard | Repair of the defective part or replacement with a new or remanufactured Audi Genuine Part, free of charge; a replacement high-voltage battery provides an SOH of at least 70% and may not be of an "as new" condition with an SOH of 100%[^1][^2] |
| Clock starts | The in-service date[^1][^2] |
| Who may repair it | An Audi dealer designated as an Audi e-tron certified dealer, with a certified technician[^1][^2] |
| Where it is valid | Vehicles originally sold by an Audi dealer in the United States, including its territories; applicable only in the United States and its territories[^1][^2] |

## What is not covered

Audi's 2025 and 2027 booklets exclude these, worded the same in both:

- Damage or malfunction caused by improper charging of the High Voltage System.[^5]
- Damage or malfunction caused by improper storage or handling of the high voltage vehicle, including parking the vehicle for more than 24 hours when the ambient temperature is higher than 118 °F (48 °C).[^5]
- Damage or malfunction caused by operation of the Audi e-tron vehicle in areas flooded with water higher than the bottom of the vehicle body.[^5]
- The replacement of any Audi e-tron high voltage system components that wear as a result of normal wear or deterioration, driving habits, or driving conditions.[^5]
- Damage due to failure to take the vehicle to an Audi e-tron dealer upon discovery of a defect in manufacturer's material or workmanship.[^5]
- Service, repair, or any damage resulting from services or repairs performed at a facility that is not an Audi e-tron certified dealer by a non-certified technician.[^6]
- Vehicles used for commercial purposes including, but not limited to, taxi, courier or delivery service, or limousine; if a commercial vehicle is sold to a subsequent retail owner, the warranty still does not apply.[^5]
- The warranty is void if the vehicle identification number or odometer has been altered, or the actual mileage cannot be determined, and when a vehicle is reported as dismantled, fire/flood damaged, junked, rebuilt, reconstructed, salvaged, or declared a total loss by an insurer.[^5]
