# Nissan Rogue Plug-in Hybrid: Battery Warranty

## Lithium-ion battery coverage

| Model year | Coverage | Term |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Plug-In Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV) System Coverage | 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^2] |

- This coverage covers any repairs needed to correct defects in materials or workmanship.[^2]
- PHEV System components: Lithium-Ion Battery, HPCM/VCM, Lithium-Ion Battery Controller, Inverter, DC/DC Converter, Motor, Gear Box, Lithium-Ion Battery Cooling System and INV Cooling System.[^2]

## Coverage at a glance

Model year 2026:

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Vehicles it applies to | A Nissan vehicle originally distributed by Nissan, originally sold by an authorized Nissan dealership in the United States, and registered in the U.S. and normally operated in the United States, the U.S. territories and Canada[^3] |
| Transfers to a later owner | Yes, without any action on the owner's part; the warranty is instead void if, in the first six months after delivery to the original owner, ownership is transferred and the vehicle is registered outside the United States[^3] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is delivered to the first retail buyer or put into use, whichever is earlier[^4] |
| Replacement parts | Genuine Nissan or Nissan approved new or remanufactured parts[^5] |
| Cost to the owner | No charge for parts and/or labor[^5] |
| Where warranty service is done | An authorized Nissan dealer in the United States or Canada, with the vehicle brought there at the owner's expense[^5] |
| Towing | To the nearest authorized Nissan dealership, when the vehicle is inoperative due to the failure of a warranted part[^2] |
| Data access | The owner is required to consent to give Nissan access to data stored on vehicle systems; failure to do so is likely to result in the denial of warranty coverage[^5] |

## What is not covered

The New Vehicle Limited Warranty, which carries the PHEV System Coverage, does not cover damage, failures or corrosion resulting from or caused by:[^6]

- Failure to operate the vehicle in accordance with the Owner's Manual.
- Misuse, such as using the vehicle to tow.
- Failure of a component not covered by warranty.
- Using the vehicle as a power source with equipment not authorized by Nissan as outlined in the Owner's Manual.
- Racing and competitive driving.
- Theft, vandalism, fire, or flooding (including engine water ingestion).
- Accident, collision, being towed.
- Repairs performed by anyone other than an authorized Nissan dealer.
- Glass breakage, unless resulting from defects in material or workmanship.
- Normal wear and tear, including dings, dents, chips, or scratches.
- Damage caused by any automated or manually operated car wash or by using a pressure washer.
- Alteration, tampering, or improper repair.
- Installation of non-Nissan approved accessories or components.
- Improper installation of any Nissan approved aftermarket accessory or component.
- A vehicle whose odometer mileage has been altered, or the odometer repaired or replaced and the actual vehicle mileage cannot be correctly and readily determined.
- Stone chipping, chemical fallout (acid rain), tree sap, salt, hail, windstorm, lightning, flood or other environmental conditions.
- Under corrosion coverage (perforation from corrosion): exhaust system components; corrosion of outer trim parts, such as moldings, which is instead warranted for 12 months or 12,500 miles; corrosion other than perforation, which is covered under the Basic Coverage; and special bodies or equipment not manufactured or supplied by Nissan.
- Lack or performance of proper maintenance services as outlined in the Owner's Manual.
- Fuel, fluids, or lubricants that are contaminated or that do not meet the specification as outlined in the Owner's Manual.
- Use of parts not equivalent in quality or design to parts supplied by Nissan.

The same warranty also excludes:

- Incidental or consequential damages such as loss of the use of the vehicle, inconvenience or commercial loss.[^4]
- Embedded or installed software and/or firmware, and updates to it, which is licensed, not sold.[^6]
- Complaints related to failure to comply with proper use of the vehicle as described in the Owner's Manual, or to the vehicle's lack of compliance with local regulations or environmental requirements of any country other than the U.S. or Canada.[^6]
- Any vehicle that is, or ever has been, issued a "salvage" or similar title, including junk, scrap, rebuilt or flood titles, or that has ever been determined a "total loss" by an insurance company; the warranty is void for such a vehicle.[^6]
- Normal maintenance services such as cleaning and polishing, wheel alignment, headlight aiming, replacement of filters, replacement of windshield wiper inserts, lubricants, coolant, worn brake shoes, pads, drums and rotors and worn clutch discs.[^6]
