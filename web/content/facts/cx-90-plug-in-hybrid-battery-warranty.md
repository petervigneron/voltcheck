# Mazda CX-90 Plug-in Hybrid: Battery Warranty

## High Voltage Battery Limited Warranty

| Model year | Term | Booklet edition read |
|---|---|---|
| 2024[^4] | 96 months (8 years) or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] | Edition 1, November 2023[^1] |
| 2025[^5] | 96 months (8 years) or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] | Edition 2, August 2024[^2] |
| 2026[^6] | 96 months (8 years) or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3] | Edition 1, December 2025[^3] |

## Longer battery term for a 2024 or 2025 car certified as a TZEV and currently registered in a listed state

| Model year | High voltage battery, TZEV Emission Warranty | States |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] | California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Vermont[^1] |
| 2025 | 10 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] | California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Vermont, Virginia, Washington[^2] |

- This term sits in the California Emission Control Warranty, whose TZEV Emission Warranty parts list includes the high voltage battery.[^1][^2]
- Mazda's 2024 booklet names the CX-70 PHEV and CX-90 PHEV as certified TZEV; its 2025 booklet names the Plug In Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV) Model.[^1][^2]

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The High Voltage Battery, for defects in materials and workmanship[^1][^2][^3] |
| Clock starts | The Date of First Service: the first date the vehicle is delivered to the first retail purchaser or lessee, or is placed into service as a company vehicle (for example a demonstrator, rental or fleet vehicle)[^1][^2][^3] |
| Repair standard | An Authorized Mazda Dealer repairs or replaces the defective High Voltage Battery without charge for parts or labor, using a new, remanufactured or factory reconditioned High Voltage Battery with a suitable energy capacity level at the time of warranty repair[^1][^2][^3] |
| Transfers to a later owner | Yes, during the warranty period: the battery warranty's Warranty Application is the same as the New Vehicle Limited Warranty's, under which the warranty is transferable to subsequent owners during the warranty period[^1][^2][^3] |
| Where to get service | A Mazda Dealer in the United States, Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, Saipan, American Samoa, Canada or Mexico[^1][^2][^3] |
| Outside Mazda's liability | Any expense of, or payment for loss of use of, the vehicle during warranty repairs[^1][^2][^3] |

## What is not covered

Mazda's 2024, 2025 and 2026 booklets list under the High Voltage Battery Limited Warranty's "What Is Not Covered":

- Any damage caused by any condition listed under "What Is Not Covered" in the New Vehicle Limited Warranty.[^1][^2][^3]
- Any damage caused by failures to follow Manufacturer's recommendations including the Owner's Manual in the use, charging, storage and servicing of the High Voltage Battery.[^1][^2][^3]
