## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Later owners | Covered[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |
| Clock starts | The date of original retail delivery or original use, whichever occurs first; mileage limits are actual odometer miles[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |
| Which cars | A vehicle originally distributed by MMNA, originally sold by an EV Certified Dealership in the United States, and registered and normally operated in the United States[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |
| Where to get service | An EV Certified Dealer in the United States; a vehicle that is drivable and safe to operate must be taken there during regular business hours at the owner's expense[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] |

## What is not covered

Mitsubishi's 2018 through 2025 booklets state that the Main Drive Lithium-ion Battery Coverage does not cover damage to or failure of the battery resulting from or caused by:

- Storage in or exposure to extreme high or extreme low temperatures.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Storing the vehicle for long periods of the time without proper maintenance.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Failure to keep the battery charged during storage of the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Physical damage to the battery due to road hazards, debris, accidents or other outside factors.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Exposing the battery to a direct flame.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Immersion of the battery in water or other liquids.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Servicing of the battery by anyone other than an EV Certified Dealer.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Not following the correct charging procedures as specified in the vehicle's Owner's Manual.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Charging the vehicle using incompatible and/or incorrect charging equipment.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Delay or failure to have an existing problem repaired on the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
- Failure to follow the instructions regarding the battery specified in the vehicle's Owner's Manual.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

The New Vehicle Limited Warranty also excludes any vehicle previously declared a total loss and/or transferred or title branded as salvage, due to an accident or other catastrophic event; this exclusion does not apply to the Federal or California emission control warranties.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
