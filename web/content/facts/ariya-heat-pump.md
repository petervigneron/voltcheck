## Scope note

Model years 2023 through 2025, US market; model year 2026 is not covered. Nissan nowhere states that the US Ariya lacks a heat pump — what is stated here is that no Nissan document published for the US Ariya says it has one, and that the one Nissan list enumerating the car's high-voltage components names a PTC heater in the heating position. Non-US Ariya builds are out of scope entirely: Nissan does list a heat pump as standard equipment on the European Ariya, which is a different car by specification.
