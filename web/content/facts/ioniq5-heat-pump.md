# Hyundai Ioniq 5: Heat Pump by Model Year and Drivetrain

## Heat pump by model year and drivetrain

| Model year | Trims | RWD | AWD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | SE, SEL, Limited | None[^1] | Standard[^1] |
| 2023 | SE, SEL, Limited | None[^2] | Standard[^2] |
| 2024 | SE, SEL, Limited | None[^3] | Standard[^3] |
| 2025 | SE, SEL, XRT, Limited | None[^4] | Standard[^4] |
| 2026 | SE, SEL, XRT, Limited | None[^5] | Standard[^5] |

## Trims whose drivetrain is fixed

- Every SE Standard Range is rear-wheel drive, so none has a heat pump.[^4]
- Every XRT is all-wheel drive — Hyundai lists an XRT range figure only in the AWD column, for 2025 and 2026.[^4][^5]

## What an RWD Ioniq 5 heats with instead

| Model years | Cabin heater |
|---|---|
| 2022–2026 | PTC heater, standard on every trim[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |

## The two builds, by refrigerant charge

| Build | Air conditioning refrigerant | Compressor lubricant |
|---|---|---|
| With heat pump | 900±25 g R-1234yf[^6][^7] | 180±10 g[^6] / 190±10 g[^7] |
| Without heat pump | 700±25 g R-1234yf[^6][^7] | 150±10 g[^6][^7] |

## Scope note

Model years 2022 through 2026, US market, each read from Hyundai's own Features grid for that model year, every Heat Pump row read from a rendered image of its page. The Ioniq 5 N is a different model and is out of scope; Hyundai's 2026 IONIQ 5 N grid marks its Heat Pump row standard.[^8] Non-US Ioniq 5 builds are out of scope entirely.
