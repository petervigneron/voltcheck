# Hyundai Ioniq 5 (model years 2022–2026, US market): Heat Pump

## The answer is the drivetrain, not the trim

Hyundai's own equipment grid gives the Heat Pump row the same value in every trim column, in every model year from 2022 through 2026: **AWD only**. It is not an option a buyer of a rear-wheel-drive Ioniq 5 could add, and it is not a reason to move up a trim.

- 2022, trims SE / SEL / Limited: "AWD only" in all three columns.[^1]
- 2023, trims SE / SEL / Limited: "AWD only" in all three columns.[^2]
- 2024, trims SE / SEL / Limited: "AWD-only" in all three columns.[^3]
- 2025, trims SE / SEL / XRT / Limited: "AWD-only" in all four columns.[^4]
- 2026, trims SE / SEL / XRT / Limited: "AWD-only" in all four columns.[^5]

Every rear-wheel-drive Ioniq 5 in those five model years is therefore a car without a heat pump, including every SE Standard Range, which Hyundai builds only as RWD.[^4]

The XRT is the one trim the rule resolves on its own: Hyundai lists an XRT range figure only in the AWD column of its range table, in both the 2025 and 2026 documents, and XRT appears in neither the RWD column nor the RWD Standard Range column of either. (In the 2026 document every range figure in that table is still a highlighted "TBD" placeholder; the column XRT sits in is not.)[^4][^5]

## What an RWD Ioniq 5 heats the cabin with instead

A PTC heater. Hyundai's grid carries a separate PTC Heater row directly above the Heat Pump row and marks it "S" — standard — in every trim column in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

## Model year 2026

The 2026 answer comes from Hyundai's own 2026 grid, not from an inference: *2026 IONIQ 5 Features and Specifications* marks the Heat Pump row "AWD-only" in all four trim columns, exactly as the 2025 document does.[^5]

Hyundai's separate 2026 Model Year Changes document agrees with it by omission. It lists the "2026 IONIQ 5 (EV)" as a **Carry-over Model**, and its table of changes from the '25 model year to the '26 names three things: the included charging cable (Level 1, changed to a Level 1 / Level 2 combo), a new Sage Silver Matte color, and two colors moving from XRT-only to all trims. No HVAC change is listed.[^6]

## Hyundai's manuals confirm the car is built both ways

Hyundai's Ioniq 5 Owner's Manual gives the air conditioning system two refrigerant charges, one for each build: "Heat pump" 900±25 g (32±0.9 oz.) and "Without heat pump" 700±25 g (25±0.9 oz.), both R-1234yf. Those two figures are identical in the 2022 manual and the 2025 manual. The compressor lubricant splits the same way but not at the same figures — 180±10 g against 150±10 g in the 2022 manual, 190±10 g against 150±10 g in the 2025 manual.[^7][^8]

## How this was checked

- Hyundai publishes two separate documents per model year, Specifications and Features, and only the Features document carries the HVAC grid. The word "pump" appears nowhere in Hyundai's 2022, 2023 or 2024 IONIQ 5 **Specifications** documents — but those documents contain no HVAC or interior section at all, so that silence is not evidence of anything and is not used here. Control: each of them does carry a "Battery Heating System" row, which is a different system.[^9]
- Every Heat Pump row quoted above was read from the PDF page rendered as an image, not from extracted text, because these are grid tables and text extraction reorders columns.
- Hyundai's press releases do not use the words. "Pump" occurs zero times in Hyundai's 2022 IONIQ 5 launch release and zero times in its September 2024 release announcing the 2025 range, against 9 and 3 occurrences of "battery" in the same searches.[^10][^11]
- Hyundai's file metadata cannot be trusted to name the model year. The 2026 grid's PDF Title field reads "2025 IONIQ 5 Product Guide", while the header printed on the page itself reads "2026 IONIQ 5 Features and Specifications" — read from the rendered image of its first page and of its HVAC page. That header is drawn rather than set as text, so a text search of the file for "2026" returns nothing at all; the file is identified here by its filename and by what its rendered pages show.[^5]

## Not covered here

The Ioniq 5 N is a different model and is not covered by any figure above. For what it is worth as a boundary marker, Hyundai's own 2026 IONIQ 5 N grid has a single column, headed "N AWD", and marks Heat Pump "S".[^12]

## Scope note

Model years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 are each checked against Hyundai's own Features grid for that year — five documents, all opened and read this pass, every Heat Pump row read from a rendered image of its page. Hyundai's 2026 Model Year Changes document is used only as a second, agreeing source for 2026, not as the basis for it.

An earlier draft of this page said no 2026 IONIQ 5 grid existed and rested 2026 on the carry-over statement alone. That was wrong: the grid exists, at 67177-2026IONIQ5SpecsFeatures20250702.pdf. It was missed because its PDF Title metadata says "2025 IONIQ 5 Product Guide" and its printed "2026" header is drawn rather than set as text, so neither a metadata check nor a text search for "2026" finds it.

"AWD only" and "AWD-only" are Hyundai's exact cell values; the hyphen appears from the 2024 document onward. Hyundai's grids print no legend defining "S" or "-", so the reading of "S" as standard follows the grid's own use of it — every car in a column carries every "S" row — rather than a printed key.

The 2025 and 2026 IONIQ 5 grids use one SE column for both the SE and the SE Standard Range, and write the difference out longhand where the two differ, as in the Trailering Package row. The Heat Pump cell is a plain "AWD-only", and the SE Standard Range is a rear-wheel-drive car, so it is excluded by the cell's own terms rather than by inference.

The 2025 IONIQ 5 Owner's Manual is a mirror hosted on cdn.dealereprocess.org, not on Hyundai's own host; it is the Hyundai-branded document, and it is used here only for a refrigerant-capacity table that the 2022 manual on Hyundai's own host states the same way.

The Ioniq 5 N is out of scope. Non-US Ioniq 5 builds are out of scope entirely; no non-US document was opened.
