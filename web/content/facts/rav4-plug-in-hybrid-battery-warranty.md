## What is not covered

Toyota's New Vehicle Limited Warranty, which carries the Hybrid Battery Warranty, does not cover damage or failures resulting directly from any of the following:[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- Fire, accidents or theft.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Abuse or negligence.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Misuse — for example, racing or overloading.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Improper repairs.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Alteration or tampering, including installation of non-Genuine Toyota Accessories.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Lack of or improper maintenance, including use of fluids and fuel other than those specified in the Owner's Manual.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Installation of parts that are not Toyota Genuine Parts.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Airborne chemicals, tree sap, road debris (including stone chips), rail dust, salt, hail, floods, wind storms, lightning and other environmental conditions.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Water contamination.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- 2025: abuse or damage to the vehicle or components that occurs as a result of participation in an HPDE/track event (High Performance Driving Event), or off-road use.[^5]
- 2026: damage to the vehicle or components that occurs as a result of abuse or misuse of the vehicle while participating in an HPDE/track event (High Performance Driving Event), or off-road use.[^6]
- 2025 and 2026: wear or damage from participating in competitive motorsports events.[^5][^6]

The original 2021 printing reads "directly or indirectly."[^1]

The warranty also does not cover:

- Tires, which are covered by a separate warranty provided by the tire manufacturer.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Noise, vibration, cosmetic conditions and other deterioration caused by normal wear and tear.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Reduction of lithium-ion battery capacity, which gradually reduces with time and use.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] On a 2026 car in California, Massachusetts, New York, Oregon, Vermont or Washington, the California Emission Control Warranty covers capacity below 70% of original for 8 years or 100,000 miles.[^6]
- Normal maintenance services such as engine tune-ups; replacement of fluids and filters; lubrication; cleaning and polishing; replacement of spark plugs and fuses; and replacement of worn wiper blades, brake pads/linings and clutch linings.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Failure of a vehicle on which the odometer has been altered so that actual vehicle mileage cannot be readily determined.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Any vehicle that has ever been branded as salvage, total loss, true mileage unknown or similar title under any state's law, or has ever been declared a "total loss" or equivalent by a financial institution or insurer. This exclusion does not apply to the emission control warranties or to any open Safety Recalls/SSCs/LSCs.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- 2022 through 2026: software updates made available for download by owners directly, either on a complimentary basis or for a fee, as determined by Toyota in its sole discretion. This exclusion does not apply to the emission control warranties.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Incidental or consequential damages associated with a vehicle failure.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
