## What is not covered

Among the exclusions in the warranty for 2022, 2023 and 2024, purchased or leased before April 16, 2024, damage caused by:

- Immersion/flooding of the high voltage battery assembly.[^1]
- Abuse and/or misuse of the vehicle and/or high voltage battery pack, such as driving over curbs, overloading, racing or track use.[^1]
- Failure to observe and resolve vehicle warnings and indications of battery concerns within a reasonable amount of time.[^1]
- The use of incompatible charging devices or methods.[^1]
- Modifications to the vehicle, high voltage battery assembly, high voltage system, or associated wiring.[^1]
- Allowing the high voltage battery assembly to remain in a fully discharged, or near zero state of charge, for more than 14 days.[^1]
- Improper vehicle storage resulting in high voltage battery damage.[^1]

Also in the warranty for 2022, 2023 and 2024, purchased or leased before April 16, 2024:

- Lucid vehicles being used to provide taxi or ride sharing services are not covered.[^1]

Among the exclusions in the warranties for 2024 and 2025 purchased or leased on or after April 16, 2024, and 2026 and 2027, damage, conditions, or problems caused by:

- The use of non-recommended or incompatible charging devices or methods.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Alteration by non-Lucid personnel to the high voltage battery assembly, high voltage system, or associated wiring.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Failure to properly store the vehicle as described in the Battery Information section of the Owner's Manual that results in damage to the high voltage battery.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Allowing the high voltage battery to discharge to a 0% state of charge or 0 miles/0km of range. In some cases this may result in an irreparable reduction in capacity below 70% or even require replacement of the high voltage battery, and a replacement battery would not be covered under warranty.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

In the battery warranty itself, all years:

- Failure to follow the recommended guidelines and charging procedures in the owner's manual is not covered.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Loss of energy or power reduction over time is not covered beyond the terms and limits of the warranty.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

Performance metrics:

- 2022, 2023 and 2024, purchased or leased before April 16, 2024: performance metrics advertised by Lucid and/or other testing agencies are not covered.[^1]
- 2024 and 2025 purchased or leased on or after April 16, 2024, and 2026 and 2027: performance metrics advertised by Lucid and/or available from third-party testing agencies, for example EPA estimated range, are not guaranteed or covered.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
