# Hyundai Ioniq 6: Heat Pump by Model Year and Trim

## Heat pump by model year and trim

| Model year | Trim | Heat pump |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | SE Standard Range | None[^1] |
| 2023 | SE Long Range | Standard[^1] |
| 2023 | SEL | Standard[^1] |
| 2023 | Limited | Standard[^1] |
| 2024 | SE Standard Range | None[^2] |
| 2024 | SE Long Range | Standard[^2] |
| 2024 | SEL | Standard[^2] |
| 2024 | Limited | Standard[^2] |
| 2025 | SE Standard Range | None[^3] |
| 2025 | SE | Standard[^3] |
| 2025 | SEL | Standard[^3] |
| 2025 | Limited | Standard[^3] |

- The 53.0 kWh Standard Range battery is sold only as an SE.[^4][^5]
- On SEL and Limited the heat pump is standard with either drivetrain; Hyundai attaches no drivetrain condition to those cells.[^1][^2][^3]

## Scope note

US model years 2023, 2024 and 2025, read from Hyundai's own features grid for each year. Model year 2026 is not covered: Hyundai calls it a Product Enhancement Model with details to come and has published no features grid for it.[^6] The Ioniq 6 N is a different model and is out of scope, as are non-US builds.
