# Kia Sportage Plug-in Hybrid: Battery Warranty

## Hybrid battery coverage

| Model year | Hybrid System Warranty, all states | California Emissions Vehicles |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 10 years or 100,000 miles[^1] | 10 years or 150,000 miles, excluding Pennsylvania and Washington[^1] |
| 2024 | 10 years or 100,000 miles[^2] | 10 years or 150,000 miles, excluding Pennsylvania and Washington[^2] |
| 2025 | 10 years or 100,000 miles[^3] | 10 years or 150,000 miles, excluding Pennsylvania and Washington[^3] |
| 2026 | 10 years or 100,000 miles[^4] | Emission Warranty Parts List: 10 years or 100,000 miles. Warranted to deteriorate to no less than 70% battery state of health: 8 years or 100,000 miles[^4] |
| 2027 | 10 years or 100,000 miles[^5] | Emission Warranty Parts List: 10 years or 100,000 miles. Warranted to deteriorate to no less than 70% battery state of health: 8 years or 100,000 miles[^5] |

- Every term above ends at whichever limit comes first.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- 2023 through 2025: the California Emission Control Warranty parts list marks the Hybrid Battery Pack Assy "10/150", 10 years or 150,000 miles, and "10/100", 10 years or 100,000 miles, in Pennsylvania and Washington.[^1][^2][^3]
- 2026 and 2027: the hybrid system warranty for California emission vehicles is subject to the California emission control warranty on printed p. 30. That warranty's parts list marks the Hybrid Battery Pack Assy "10/100", 10 years or 100,000 miles, and its note 1 warrants the listed parts of a Sportage plug-in hybrid, except the high voltage battery, for 15 years or 150,000 miles, with the exception of Pennsylvania.[^4][^5]
- 2026 and 2027: the California Emission Control Warranty Statement in the same manual, on printed pp. 25 and 27, says that all parts of a plug-in hybrid listed on the Emission Warranty Parts List are warranted for 15 years or 150,000 miles. The parts list on printed p. 30 gives the Hybrid Battery Pack Assy 10 years or 100,000 miles.[^4][^5]
- 2026 and 2027: the coverage chart on printed p. 3 lists, under California Emissions Defect, "PHEV HV battery" at 96 months or 100,000 miles and "PHEV" at 180 months or 150,000 miles.[^4][^5]

## What is not covered

The Hybrid System Warranty's exclusions are those of the "What is Not Covered" section of the Kia New Vehicle Limited Warranty, whose headings, in order, cover:[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

- Damage due to factors beyond the manufacturer's control, such as misuse (including racing and overloading), accidents (including collision, fire and theft), alteration, modification, tampering or rewiring, and damage or corrosion from the environment, including floods.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Damage due to lack of maintenance, or to the use of other than the specified fuel, oil or lubricants recommended in the Owner's Manual.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Manufacturer design choices, including the materials chosen for parts and components.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Normal deterioration: parts intended to wear, and surface corrosion on parts other than the exterior body sheet metal panels.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Normal maintenance services and items.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Any repair of a vehicle whose odometer has been altered or whose actual mileage cannot be reasonably determined.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Economic loss and other incidental, special, consequential or exemplary damages, including loss of use, lodging, car rentals, travel costs and loss of pay.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Tires, which are warranted by the tire manufacturers.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Any vehicle that has ever been or should have been issued a "salvage" title or similar "branded" title under any state's law, or has been declared a "total loss" or equivalent by a financial institution or insurance company. This exclusion does not apply to Emission Warranties, the Replacement Parts Limited Warranty or any recalls or campaigns.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
- Production changes: Kia and its Authorized Kia Dealers reserve the right to make changes in vehicles at any time without incurring any obligation to make the same or similar changes on vehicles previously built or sold.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

The Hybrid System Warranty also excludes incidental or consequential damages, including without limitation loss of time, inconvenience, loss of use of the vehicle, or commercial loss.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
