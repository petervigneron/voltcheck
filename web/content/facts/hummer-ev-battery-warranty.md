# GMC Hummer EV Pickup and Hummer EV SUV: Battery Warranty

## Model years

| Model | Model years |
|---|---|
| Hummer EV Pickup | 2022[^14], 2023[^15], 2024[^16], 2025[^16], 2026[^17] |
| Hummer EV SUV | 2024[^15][^16], 2025[^16], 2026[^17] |

## Battery coverage by model year

| Model year | Booklet read | Term | Capacity floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | First printing, 15835627A (September 2021) | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] | Replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 60% of its original value during the warranty period[^1] |
| 2022 | December 2024 edition, 15835627B | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3] | Replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 75% of its original value during the warranty period[^3] |
| 2023 | December 2024 edition, 16589939B | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^5] | Replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 75% of its original value during the warranty period[^5] |
| 2024 | January 2025 edition, 17267651C | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^7] | Replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 75% of its original value during the warranty period[^7] |
| 2025 | 18455115A (May 2024) | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^9] | Replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 75% of its original value during the warranty period[^9] |
| 2026 | 19680588B | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^11] | Replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 75% of its original value during the warranty period[^11] |

GMC's manuals page lists both 2022 printings for the 2022 Hummer EV.[^13]

## Coverage at a glance

Model years 2022 through 2026:

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered, 2022 and 2023 | Propulsion battery pack and all internal components[^1][^3][^5] |
| What is covered, 2024 | Defects related to materials or workmanship to the propulsion battery pack and its internal components[^7] |
| What is covered, 2025 and 2026 | The propulsion battery pack and its internal components[^9][^11] |
| Who judges the capacity floor | A certified dealer[^1][^3][^5][^7][^9][^11] |
| Battery fitted below the floor | One appropriate for the age and mileage of the vehicle[^1][^3][^5][^7][^9][^11] |
| Repair parts | New or refurbished components, at GMC's discretion, for a propulsion battery that needs service due to a defect in materials or workmanship[^1][^3][^5][^7][^9][^11] |
| Where repairs are done, 2022 through 2024 | An EV Certified GMC dealer facility[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Where repairs are done, 2025 and 2026 | An authorized GMC dealership[^9][^11] |
| Cost to the owner | Warranty repairs, including towing, parts and labor, at no charge[^1][^3][^5][^7][^9][^11] |
| Towing, 2022 through 2024 | To the nearest EV Certified GMC dealer during the 8 years or 100,000 miles, if the vehicle cannot be driven because of a warranted Electric specific defect[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is first delivered or put in use[^1][^3][^5][^7][^9][^11] |

## Transfer to a later owner

| Model year | Answer |
|---|---|
| 2022 | Provided to the original and any subsequent owners during the warranty period; the electric vehicle coverage is transferable at no cost to any subsequent owner within the 8 years or 100,000 miles, with no deductibles[^1][^3] |
| 2023 (December 2024 edition) | Transferable at no cost to any subsequent owner within the 8 years or 100,000 miles, with no deductibles; "Certain Electric Vehicle Propulsion Coverages will be VOIDED if ownership of the vehicle is transferred from the original owner within the first 6 months after delivery."[^5] |
| 2024 (January 2025 edition) | Transferable at no cost to any subsequent owner within the 8 years or 100,000 miles, with no deductibles; "For Hummer EV only: Certain Electric Vehicle Propulsion Coverages will be VOIDED if ownership of the vehicle is transferred from the original owner within the first 6 months after delivery."[^7] |
| 2025 | Provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners during the warranty period; "For Hummer EV only: Coverage will be voided if ownership of the vehicle is transferred from the original owner within the first 6 months after delivery."[^9] |
| 2026 | Provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners during the warranty period; "For Hummer EV only: Coverage will be voided if ownership of the vehicle is transferred from the original owner within the first 6 months after delivery."[^11] |

## Using the pack for something other than driving

Model years 2025 and 2026:

| Covered | Not covered |
|---|---|
| Normal use for backup power during outages, reasonably selling energy back to the electrical grid, and reasonable use as a mobile power source (job site power or vehicle to vehicle charging)[^9][^11] | Excessive use for non-propulsion purposes, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions; General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use that is determined to be harmful to the battery[^10][^12] |

## What is not covered

Model years 2022 through 2026. The booklets' exclusions include:

- Damage caused by collision, fire, theft, freezing, vandalism, riot, explosion, or objects striking the vehicle.[^2][^4][^6][^8][^10][^12]
- Damage caused by alteration, modification, or tampering to the vehicle, including the body, chassis, software, or other components after final assembly.[^2][^4][^6][^8][^10][^12]
- Damage caused by water or fluid contamination.[^2][^4][^6][^8][^10][^12]
- Vehicles where the odometer has been disconnected, its reading altered, or mileage cannot be determined.[^2][^4][^6][^8][^10][^12]
- Vehicles currently or previously titled as salvaged, scrapped, junked, or otherwise considered a total loss: the warranty is void.[^2][^4][^6][^8][^10][^12]

Model years 2025 and 2026:

- Damage to the vehicle's battery from incompatible charging methods, or from an adapter that is not sold or approved by General Motors.[^10][^12]
- Damage resulting from failure to install Over-the-Air software updates; the owner is responsible for ensuring all GMC provided Over-the-Air software updates are installed within 45 days of software availability to the vehicle.[^10][^12]
