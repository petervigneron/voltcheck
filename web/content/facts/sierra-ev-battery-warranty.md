# GMC Sierra EV: Battery Warranty

## Model years

| Model year | Warranty booklet |
|---|---|
| 2024[^9] | *GMC Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information*, 2024 (January 2025 printing)[^1] |
| 2025[^10] | *GMC Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information*, 2025 (May 2024 printing)[^3] |
| 2026[^10] | *2026 GMC Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information*, edition 19680588B[^5] |
| 2027[^10] | *2027 GMC Electric Vehicle Limited Warranty and Owner Assistance Information* (October 2026 printing)[^7] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The propulsion battery pack and its internal components[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Term | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Capacity floor | 75% of its original value; the battery is replaced or repaired if capacity falls below it during the warranty period, as determined by a certified dealer[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Replacement battery | One appropriate for the age and mileage of the vehicle[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Repair parts | New or refurbished components, at GMC's discretion, for a propulsion battery that needs service due to a defect in materials or workmanship[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Where repairs are done | 2024: an EV Certified GMC dealer facility[^1]. 2025, 2026, 2027: an authorized GMC dealership[^3][^5][^7] |
| Cost to the owner | Towing, parts and labor at no charge[^1][^3][^5][^7] |
| Transfers to a later owner | Yes. The warranty is provided to the original and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period[^1][^3][^5][^7]. 2024: the battery warranty is transferable at no cost to any subsequent person who assumes ownership of the vehicle within the 8 years or 100,000 miles term, with no deductibles[^1] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is first delivered or put in use[^1][^3][^5][^7]. 2024: the battery term runs from the original in-service date of the vehicle[^1] |
| Vehicles it applies to | 2024: GMC vehicles registered in the United States and normally operated in the United States[^1]. 2025, 2026, 2027: GMC vehicles originally sold, registered, and normally operated in the United States[^3][^5][^7] |
| Bumper-to-bumper coverage | 3 years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^3][^5][^7] |

## Using the pack for something other than driving

Model years 2025, 2026 and 2027:

| Covered | Not covered |
|---|---|
| Normal use for backup power during outages, reasonably selling energy back to the electrical grid, and reasonable use as a mobile power source (job site power or vehicle to vehicle charging)[^3][^5][^7] | Excessive use for non-propulsion purposes, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions; General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use that is determined to be harmful to the battery[^4][^6][^8] |

## What is not covered

The booklets' exclusions include:

- 2025 and 2026: damage to the vehicle's battery from incompatible charging methods, or from an adapter that is not sold or approved by General Motors. 2027: incompatible charging methods, or an adapter or charger that is not sold or approved by General Motors.[^4][^6][^8]
- 2025, 2026 and 2027: damage resulting from failure to install Over-the-Air software updates; the owner is responsible for ensuring all GMC provided Over-the-Air software updates are installed within 45 days of software availability to the vehicle.[^4][^6][^8]
- Alteration, modification, or tampering to the vehicle, including, but not limited to, the body, chassis, software, or other components after final assembly.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Non-GM parts, control module calibrations, software modifications, and other alterations not issued through General Motors: they void the coverage for the components they damage or otherwise affect.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Damage from aftermarket vehicle propulsion enhancement products or modifications, which may not be covered.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Damage caused by collision, fire, theft, freezing, vandalism, riot, explosion, or objects striking the vehicle.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Water or fluid contamination.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Damage caused by failure to follow the recommended maintenance schedule intervals.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Vehicles where the odometer has been disconnected, its reading altered, or mileage cannot be determined.[^2][^4][^6][^8]
- Vehicles currently or previously titled as salvaged, scrapped, junked, or otherwise considered a total loss: the warranty is void.[^2][^4][^6][^8]

## Scope note

US-market GMC Sierra EV, model years 2024 through 2027, each read from the GMC electric-vehicle warranty booklet GM's manuals index files against the Sierra EV for that year; the booklets also carry terms marked "For Hummer EV only," which are left out. The booklet delivered with a specific car is the authority for that car.
