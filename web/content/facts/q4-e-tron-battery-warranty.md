# Audi Q4 e-tron: Battery Warranty

## High-voltage battery warranty

| Model year | Term |
|---|---|
| 2022[^7] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (part no. 8V1012723SZ, edition 2, cover dated 09.2021)[^1] |
| 2023[^7] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (part no. 8V1012723TF, edition 2, cover dated 12.2022)[^2] |
| 2024[^7] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (part no. 8V1012723TH, February 2023 edition)[^3] |
| 2025[^7] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (part no. 8V1012723TM, edition 2, cover dated 02.2025)[^4] |
| 2026[^7] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (part no. 8V1012723TQ)[^5] |

## Capacity and replacement battery

| Item | 2022–2023 | 2024–2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity coverage | Repair to correct a manufacturer's defect in material or workmanship when the remaining energy content of the battery is below 70% compared to the condition at the time of delivery, within 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^1][^2] | Repair to correct a manufacturer's defect in material or workmanship, or when the remaining energy content of the battery SOH (State of Health) is below 70% compared to the condition at the time of delivery, within 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^3][^4][^5] |
| Replacement battery | Provides the vehicle with a net capacity level of at least 70%; it may not be of an "as new" condition with 100% net capacity[^1][^2] | Provides an SOH of at least 70%; it may not be of an "as new" condition with an SOH of 100%[^3][^4][^5] |

## Coverage at a glance, 2022–2026

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The Audi e-tron high voltage battery and battery module[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| Repair standard | Repair of the defective part or replacement with a new or remanufactured Audi Genuine Part, free of charge[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| Clock starts | The in-service date[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| Who may repair it | An Audi dealer designated as an Audi e-tron certified dealer, with a certified technician[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |

## What is not covered

Among the exclusions in Audi's 2022 through 2026 booklets, worded the same in each:

- Damage or malfunction caused by improper charging of the High Voltage System.[^8]
- Damage or malfunction caused by improper storage or handling of the high voltage vehicle, including parking the vehicle for more than 24 hours when the ambient temperature is higher than 118 °F (48 °C).[^8]
- Damage or malfunction caused by operation of the Audi e-tron vehicle in areas flooded with water higher than the bottom of the vehicle body.[^8]
- The replacement of any Audi e-tron high voltage system components that wear as a result of normal wear or deterioration, driving habits, or driving conditions.[^8]
- Damage due to failure to take the vehicle to an Audi e-tron dealer upon discovery of a defect in manufacturer's material or workmanship.[^8]
- Service, repair, or any damage resulting from services or repairs performed at a facility that is not an Audi e-tron certified dealer by a non-certified technician.[^9]
- Vehicles used for commercial purposes including, but not limited to, taxi, courier or delivery service, or limousine; if a commercial vehicle is sold to a subsequent retail owner, the warranty still does not apply.[^8]
- The warranty is void if the vehicle identification number or odometer has been altered, or the actual mileage cannot be determined, and when a vehicle is reported as dismantled, fire/flood damaged, junked, rebuilt, reconstructed, salvaged, or declared a total loss by an insurer.[^8]
