# Cadillac Optiq: Charging

## Pack

| Model year | Energy | Chemistry |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 85 kWh Useable Battery Energy[^2] | Lithium-ion, NCMA cathode, blended graphite anode, 10 modules[^2] |
| 2026 | 85 kWh battery capacity[^3] | |

## Charge port

| Model year | Port |
|---|---|
| 2025 | Combined Charging System (CCS) Type 1 — the J1772 Type 1 plug combined with two high-speed DC fast-charging pins[^1][^6] |
| 2026 | J3400 North American Charging Standard (NACS), standard, supporting Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 high-speed DC fast charging[^3][^4] |

## DC fast charging

| Model year | Drivetrain | Maximum charge speed | Range added in about 10 minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Dual Motor AWD[^2] | 150 kW[^1] | 79 miles[^1][^2] |
| 2026 | Single Motor RWD[^3] | 150 kW[^3] | 81 miles[^3][^9] |
| 2026 | Dual Motor AWD[^3] | 150 kW[^3] | |

- Charging slows down as the battery fills up.[^6]
- GM recommends charging the high voltage battery to 80% or less unless the drive requires a full charge, and avoiding letting it fall below 20%.[^6]
- At a DC charge station the station's cable must be less than 10 m (33 ft) long to meet functionality and regulatory requirements.[^6]

## Adapters

| Model year | Situation | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Tesla Superchargers | A GM NACS DC adapter plugs into the vehicle's CCS charge port inlet and lets a CCS vehicle use the Supercharger network; public charging is set up in the myCadillac mobile app[^7] |
| 2025 | Which Superchargers | Select generation 3 (V3) and beyond[^7] |
| 2025 | Tesla Level 2 chargers | The NACS DC adapter is a DC adapter for Superchargers only[^7] |
| 2026 | CCS DC fast chargers | A NACS-to-CCS adapter, available as an accessory[^5] |
| 2026 | Non-NACS home and public chargers | A GM-approved adapter; GM names a J-1772 cable for Level 1 and Level 2 charging on a NACS-native car[^3][^4][^5] |
| Both | Third-party adapters | Use only adapters sold, provided or approved by General Motors. Battery damage from an incompatible charging method or an unapproved adapter is excluded from warranty coverage[^6][^8] |
