## Charging equipment

| Model year | Included when bought new |
|---|---|
| 2024 | One of three packages, in the MSRP: a Level 2 Home Charging Station with a $500 Honda Home Electrification installation credit and a $100 EVgo credit; a Level 1 and Level 2 Portable Charging Kit with a $250 installation credit and a $300 EVgo credit; or $750 in EVgo credit. All three carry 60 kWh of Electrify America introductory charging, which Honda offers to first-time Honda EV owners[^4] |
| 2025 | One of three packages, in the MSRP: a Level 2 Home Charging Station with a $500 Honda Home Electrification installation credit and a $100 EVgo credit; a Level 1 and Level 2 Portable Charging Kit with a $250 installation credit and a $300 EVgo credit; or $750 in EVgo credit. All three carry 60 kWh of Electrify America introductory charging, which Honda offers to first-time Honda EV owners[^5] |
| 2026 | The Portable Charging Kit is standard equipment. Buyers then take either a complimentary Home Charging Station with a $1,250 installation credit, or a $500 credit toward the purchase or lease. Installing through Honda Home Electrification adds Honda's Home Charging Station Extended Service Warranty[^6] |

## Connector and adapters

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Charge port | CCS[^7] |
| Tesla Supercharger access | The Honda-approved NACS-CCS DC fast-charging adapter, available since July 22, 2025, reaches more than 23,500 selected Tesla Superchargers in the United States[^8] |
| Where to buy the adapter | Authorized Honda and Acura dealers in the U.S., and the Honda DreamShop website[^8] |
| Adapter price | $225 MSRP[^8] |
| Adapter warranty | 1 year[^7] |
| What the adapter connects to | NACS J3400 DC fast-charging stations, attached to the vehicle's CCS charge port[^7] |
| Starting a Supercharger session | The Tesla app is required to begin charging; Google built-in Maps in the vehicle locates authorized Supercharger sites[^8] |
| Unapproved adapters | Honda recommends only approved adapters at Tesla Supercharger stations, and warns that damage from an unapproved adapter may not be covered under the vehicle's limited warranty[^8] |

## Scope note

US retail Prologues, model years 2024 through 2026 — the model's full run to date. Honda bases its ten-minute range figure on available public information about charging station capability along a selected route, and states that actual charge times and range vary with battery condition, charger output, vehicle settings and outside temperature.
