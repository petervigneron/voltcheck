# Lucid Gravity: Battery Warranty

## High voltage battery coverage

| Model year | Term | Capacity |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] | Minimum 70% retention of battery capacity over the warranty period[^1] |
| 2026 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] | Minimum 70% retention of battery capacity over the warranty period[^2] |
| 2027 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3] | Minimum 70% retention of battery capacity over the warranty period[^3] |

- If the high voltage battery falls below 70% capacity during the warranty period, as determined at the sole discretion of Lucid's trained and authorized representatives, Lucid will replace the high voltage battery.[^1][^2][^3]
- The measurement method used to determine the battery capacity is at the sole discretion of Lucid's trained and authorized representatives.[^1][^2][^3]

## Zero-Emission Vehicle (ZEV) Limited Warranties, 2026 and 2027

| Model year | Coverage | Term |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Battery free from defects in materials and workmanship which cause the battery state of health to deteriorate to less than 70%[^2] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^2] |
| 2027 | Battery free from defects in materials and workmanship which cause the battery state of health to deteriorate to less than 70%[^3] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^3] |

- Lucid provides its Zero-Emission Vehicle Limited Warranties only to vehicles certified for sale in California and registered in a state that, at the time of delivery and the applicable claim, has adopted and is enforcing California's Advanced Clean Cars II regulations.[^2][^3]
- Under the Propulsion-Related Part Warranty, high-priced propulsion-related parts, among them the High-Voltage Battery Assembly & Battery Management System and the Battery Management Unit (BMU) + Battery Disconnect Unit (BDU), are covered for 7 years or 70,000 miles, whichever first occurs.[^2][^3]

## What is not covered

Among the exclusions in the 2025, 2026 and 2027 warranties, damage, conditions, or problems caused by:

- The use of non-recommended or incompatible charging devices or methods.[^1][^2][^3]
- Alteration by non-Lucid personnel to the high voltage battery assembly, high voltage system, or associated wiring.[^1][^2][^3]
- Failure to properly store the vehicle as described in the Battery Information section of the Owner's Manual that results in damage to the high voltage battery.[^1][^2][^3]
- Allowing the high voltage battery to discharge to a 0% state of charge or 0 miles/0km of range. In some cases this may result in an irreparable reduction in capacity below 70% or even require replacement of the high voltage battery, and a replacement battery would not be covered under warranty.[^1][^2][^3]

In the battery warranty itself:

- Malfunctions or problems caused by failure to follow the recommended guidelines and charging procedures in the owner's manual are not covered.[^1][^2][^3]
- Loss of energy or power reduction over time is not covered beyond the terms and limits of the warranty.[^1][^2][^3]
- Performance metrics advertised by Lucid and/or available from third-party testing agencies, for example EPA estimated range, are not guaranteed or covered.[^1][^2][^3]

## What voids the warranty

| Event | 2025 and 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| The vehicle sold, designated, labeled or branded as dismantled, fire-damaged, flood-damaged, junk, rebuilt, salvage, reconstructed, irreparable, or a total loss, including an insurance carrier's total-loss determination | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion[^1][^2] | Voided, and no warranty coverage provided[^3] |
| Failing to comply with any recall notice | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion[^1][^2] | Voided, and no warranty coverage provided[^3] |
| Defacing or altering the VIN or odometer, or any related system, so that the VIN or actual mileage is difficult to determine | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion[^1][^2] | Voided, and no warranty coverage provided[^3] |
| Failing to install required software updates within 30 days after notification that an update is available | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion[^1][^2] | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion[^3] |
| Use of the vehicle for taxi, rental, or for-compensation ride-sharing services | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion[^1][^2] | Voided at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion; the 2027 list covers taxi, limo, rental and ride-sharing services[^3] |

- Where voiding is at Lucid's sole and exclusive discretion, Lucid may exercise that discretion at any time after the event, and the voiding of coverage is retroactive to the time of the event.[^1][^2][^3]
