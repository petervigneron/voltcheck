# Tesla Model Y (model years 2020–2026): Heat Pump by Year

## 2020–2024

Every Model Y built for model years 2020 through 2024 has a Heat Pump Assembly, listed by Tesla among the car's High Voltage Components.[^1]

Est. Tesla's Model Y shipped with this heat pump system from its first customer deliveries in March 2020. No earlier Model Y build lacked one.[^2]

## 2025–2026

Every Model Y built for model years 2025 and 2026 has a Heat Pump Assembly, listed by Tesla among the car's High Voltage Components.[^3]

Not covered: whether the 2025–2026 Model Y Standard (base RWD) trim received the same heat pump hardware revision as other trims in that window. No source specific to that trim's hardware was found.
