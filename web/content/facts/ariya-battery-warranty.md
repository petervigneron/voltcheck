# Nissan Ariya: Battery Warranty

## Lithium-ion battery coverage

| Model year | Defects in materials or workmanship | Capacity loss |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] | Below nine segments on the battery capacity level gauge: 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] |
| 2024 | 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] | Below nine segments on the battery capacity level gauge: 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] |
| 2025 | 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3][^4] | Below nine segments on the battery capacity level gauge: 96 months or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3][^4] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Capacity floor | Nine of the 12 segments on the vehicle's battery capacity level gauge[^1][^2][^3] |
| Repair standard | Battery capacity is returned to nine segments or more, which may fall short of an "as new" condition with all 12 segments; the original battery is repaired and returned to the vehicle where possible, otherwise replaced with a new, remanufactured or factory reconditioned lithium-ion battery[^1][^2][^3] |
| EV System Coverage | 60 months or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first: Motor, Inverter unit, VCM, Reduction gear, DC/DC converter, Onboard charger, Onboard charger connector and Trickle charge cable[^1][^2][^3] |
| Transfers to later owners | Yes, at any change of ownership, without any action by the owner[^1][^2][^3] |
| Transfer exception | The warranty is void if, during the first six months after delivery to the original owner, ownership is transferred and the vehicle is registered outside the United States[^1][^2][^3] |
| Which cars | A Nissan originally sold by an authorized Nissan dealership in the United States, registered in the U.S. and normally operated in the United States (including Alaska and Hawaii) and Canada[^1][^2][^3] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is delivered to the first retail buyer or put into use, whichever is earlier[^1][^2][^3] |

## What is not covered

Nissan's lithium-ion battery exclusions, with one general exclusion and the salvage-title rule, the same in all three booklets except where a year is named:

- Exposing the vehicle to ambient temperatures above 120F (49C) for over 24 hours.[^1][^2][^3]
- Storing the vehicle in temperatures below -13F (-25C) for over seven days.[^1][^2][^3]
- Leaving the vehicle for over 14 days where the lithium-ion battery reaches a zero or near zero state of charge.[^1][^2][^3]
- Physically damaging the lithium-ion battery, or intentionally attempting to reduce its life.[^1][^2][^3]
- Exposing the lithium-ion battery to a direct flame, or immersing any portion of it in water or fluids.[^1][^2][^3]
- Opening the lithium-ion battery enclosure, or having it serviced by someone other than a Nissan Ariya certified technician (2023, 2024) or a Nissan Electric Vehicle certified technician (2025).[^1][^2][^3]
- Neglecting to follow correct charging procedures, or using incompatible charging devices.[^1][^2][^3]
- Consequential damage caused by the failure to repair an existing problem.[^1][^2][^3]
- Repairs performed by anyone other than a Nissan Ariya certified dealer (2023, 2024) or a Nissan Electric Vehicle certified dealer (2025).[^1][^2][^3]
- Any vehicle that is or ever has been issued a "salvage" or similar title, or that an insurance company has determined to be a "total loss"; the warranty does not apply to it and is void.[^1][^2][^3]

Gradual capacity loss with time and use is not covered beyond the terms and limits of the Lithium-Ion Battery Capacity Coverage.[^1][^2][^3]

## Scope note

US market, model years 2023 through 2025, one Nissan-hosted warranty booklet per year; Nissan's owner manuals page lists Ariya booklets for those three years only. The 2023 and 2024 booklets name the Ariya in their dealer and technician wording; the 2025 booklet is Nissan's single electric-vehicle edition, names no model, and is the one Nissan links for the 2025 Ariya. The booklet delivered with a specific car is the authority for that car.
