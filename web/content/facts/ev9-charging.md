## Connector and adapters

| Model year | Charge port |
|---|---|
| 2024–2025 | CCS1[^7][^8] |
| 2026 | NACS, illuminated, with a powered door[^7][^9][^11] |

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tesla Supercharger access | Kia EV owners have access to more than 21,500 DC fast chargers in the Tesla Supercharger network, via the Kia Access app; enrollment is through the Kia Charge Pass program in the app[^7] |
| Adapter for 2024 and 2025 cars | Kia gave a NACS adapter free of charge to customers taking delivery of a new 2024 or 2025 EV9 from September 4, 2024; customers who took delivery earlier buy one from an authorized Kia dealer[^8] |
| 2026 AC adapter | Standard on every trim[^9] |
| 2026 DC adapter | $0 option on every trim; standard in ZEV states[^9][^10] |
| 2026 DC adapter, factory-installed | Light Long Range, with the Light Long Range Battery Package[^10] |
| DC adapter ratings | 1,000 VDC, 350 A at 35°C with a 400 A boost for 10 minutes, 400 A maximum, Type 3R enclosure equivalent to IP67, −22°F to 122°F, UL2251[^11] |
| AC adapter ratings | 250 VAC, 80 A, Type 3R enclosure equivalent to IP67, −22°F to 122°F, UL2251[^11] |
| Third-party adapters | Use only Kia Genuine DC adapters. Kia warns that aftermarket or other manufacturers' CCS1 adapters may cause overheating, slower charging speed, or damage to the vehicle or the adapter[^11] |
| Where to buy | Kia approved CCS1 adapters are sold by Kia dealerships[^11] |
