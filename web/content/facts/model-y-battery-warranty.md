# Tesla Model Y: Battery Warranty

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Term | 8 years, both tiers[^1] |
| Mileage cap | 100,000 or 120,000 miles depending on trim; see the two tiers below[^1][^2] |
| Capacity floor | Minimum 70% retention of battery capacity over the warranty period[^1] |
| Normal degradation | Not covered; the floor is minimum 70% retention[^1][^3] |
| How capacity is measured | At "the sole discretion of Tesla"; Tesla calls the vehicle's range estimates an imperfect measure of Battery capacity[^1] |
| Replacement pack | Not necessarily new: a condition appropriate to the vehicle's age and mileage, sufficient to meet the minimum capacity for the rest of the original warranty period[^1] |
| Transfers to a second owner | Ownership Transfer: "transferable at no cost to any person(s) who subsequently and lawfully assume(s) ownership of the vehicle after the first retail purchaser within the described limitations of this New Vehicle Limited Warranty". A later owner must return to the car's Warranty Region for warranty service, wherever they bought the car; with a United States or Canada Warranty Region, service may be sought in either country[^4][^8] |
| Tesla may void the whole warranty for | A defaced VIN, a tampered odometer, a branded or unclean title, or a vehicle an insurer has declared a total loss[^5] |

## The two mileage tiers

Tesla's warranty document and its support page name the trims differently, so both lists are given.

| Coverage | Warranty document names[^1] | Support page names[^2] |
|---|---|---|
| 8 years or 100,000 miles (160,000 km), minimum 70% retention | Model Y Standard or Standard Range Plus | Model Y RWD, Model Y AWD |
| 8 years or 120,000 miles (192,000 km), minimum 70% retention | Model Y Long Range or Performance | Model Y Premium RWD, Model Y Premium AWD, Model Y Performance AWD, Model Y L Premium Launch Series |

- Read the list your car's paperwork uses; the two vocabularies do not overlap.[^1][^2]
- Drivetrain letters do not decide it: "AWD" appears in both support-page rows, attached to different trim names.[^2]
- A car whose name is in neither list is under an older warranty document; the booklet delivered with the car is the authority for it.[^2]
- Per-car coverage is in the Tesla app, on the Specs & Warranty screen's Warranty tab.[^2]

## ZEV and emissions warranties

- Tesla calls the ZEV Limited Warranties minimum coverages set by regulation, which the New Vehicle Limited Warranty may exceed; the expiration of a ZEV Limited Warranty does not diminish coverage under any other limited warranty in it, and Tesla is not obligated to provide duplicate remedies for the same issue.[^6]
- Tesla provides the Emissions Performance Warranty in compliance with federal law. The New Vehicle Limited Warranty includes the ZEV Limited Warranties, the Emissions Performance Warranty and the Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty, each as described in it.[^7]

| Coverage | Term | Gated by |
|---|---|---|
| Battery ZEV Limited Warranty | 8 years or 100,000 miles (160,000 km)[^6] | Model Year 2026 and later, and a Warranty Region of the United States and Canada[^6][^2] |
| High-Priced Propulsion-Related Parts ZEV Limited Warranty | 7 years or 70,000 miles (110,000 km)[^6] | Model Year 2026 and later, and a Warranty Region of the United States and Canada[^6][^2] |
| Emissions Performance Warranty (Emission Defect Warranty and Emission Performance Warranty) | 8 years or 80,000 miles (128,700 km), whichever comes first, for Major Emission Control Components; Tesla's Model 3/Model Y parts list names the High Voltage Battery, and covers no component within the listed systems that is not needed to charge the high voltage battery, store energy, or transmit power to move the vehicle; the period begins on delivery to the ultimate purchaser, or on the date first placed in service if the vehicle is first a "demonstrator" or "company" car[^7] | Model Year 2027 and later, and a Warranty Region of the United States and Canada[^7] |

- EPA lists 2027 Tesla Model 3 and Model Y vehicles.[^9]
- The 70% defect standard — battery capacity not falling below 70% through defects in material and workmanship — belongs to the Battery ZEV Limited Warranty, not to coverage on a pre-2026 Model Y.[^6]

## What is excluded

- Intentional actions, including abusing or destroying the vehicle or ignoring active vehicle warnings or service notifications.[^3]
- A collision or accident, other than damage from a battery fire.[^3]
- Servicing or opening the Battery or Drive Unit by non-Tesla or non-certified personnel.[^3]
- Damaging the battery, or intentionally attempting, by physical means, programming or other methods, to extend (other than as specified in the owner manual and any documentation provided by Tesla) or reduce its life; exposing it to direct flame, except a battery fire; flooding it.[^3]
- Issues resulting from failing to follow the recommended battery maintenance and charging procedures in the owner documentation.[^3]
- Noticeable performance changes caused by software updates.[^3]
- Battery fire damage is covered even from driver error, except damage sustained before the fire and any damage if the fire occurred after the vehicle had already been totaled.[^1]
