# Jeep Wrangler 4xe: Battery Warranty

## High voltage battery coverage

| Model year | Term |
|---|---|
| 2021 | 10 years or 150,000 miles in California and states that adopt California ZEV regulations; 10 years or 100,000 miles in states that do not[^1] |
| 2022 | 8 years or 100,000 miles[^2] |
| 2023 | 8 years or 100,000 miles[^3] |
| 2024 | 10 years or 150,000 miles in TZEV states; 8 years or 100,000 miles in non-TZEV states[^4] |
| 2025 | 10 years or 150,000 miles in California and the states that adopt California TZEV regulations; 8 years or 100,000 miles in states that do not[^5] |

- The 2024 booklet's coverage table gives the longer term for TZEV states; its section 2.5 B gives 10 years or 150,000 miles for a vehicle certified as a Transitional Zero Emission Vehicle and sold, registered and operated in California.[^4]
- For 2023, Jeep prints the Grand Cherokee 4xe and the Wrangler 4xe as separate lines: the Grand Cherokee 4xe gets 10 years or 150,000 miles in ZEV states, the Wrangler 4xe a single 8 years or 100,000 miles.[^3]

## States with the longer term

| Model year | Where the longer term applies |
|---|---|
| 2021 | California and states that adopt California ZEV regulations, listed in the California Emission Warranty booklet[^1] |
| 2025 | California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Vermont, Virginia, Washington[^5] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who is covered | A purchaser for use of the vehicle[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| Clock starts | The start date of the Basic Limited Warranty[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| That start date | The earlier of the date you take delivery and the date the vehicle was first put into use, for example as a dealer "demo" or an FCA US LLC company vehicle[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| Towing under this coverage | Roadside Assistance covers towing to the nearest authorized Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep or Ram dealer when the vehicle cannot be driven because a covered part has failed[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |
| Going to a different dealer, 2021 through 2024 | The owner pays the cost of the extra distance past 10 miles[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Going to a different dealer, 2025 | The owner is responsible for the cost[^5] |

- A 4xe that spent its first months as a dealer demonstrator or an FCA US LLC company vehicle started its battery clock then, not when a customer first bought it.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

## What is not covered

Jeep's 2024 and 2025 booklets exclude damage or conditions caused by:

- Physically damaging the lithium-ion battery, or intentionally attempting to reduce its life.[^4][^5]
- Leaving the vehicle for over 30 days where the lithium-ion battery reaches a zero or near zero state of charge.[^4][^5]
- Neglecting the charging procedures in the owner's manual, or using incompatible charging devices.[^4][^5]
- Opening the lithium-ion battery enclosure, or having it serviced by someone other than a certified technician at an authorized repair facility.[^4][^5]
- Exposing the lithium-ion battery to contact with a direct flame.[^4][^5]
- Exposing the vehicle to ambient temperatures above 140°F (60°C) for over 24 hours.[^4][^5]

Both booklets state that the battery will experience gradual capacity loss with time and use, and place that loss outside the High Voltage Battery Limited Warranty.[^4][^5]

## The rest of the hybrid hardware

| Model year | FCA US LLC Limited Emission Warranty |
|---|---|
| 2021 | 10 years or 100,000 miles[^1] |
| 2022 | 8 years or 100,000 miles[^2] |
| 2023 | 8 years or 100,000 miles[^3] |
| 2024 | 8 years or 100,000 miles[^4] |
| 2025 | 8 years or 100,000 miles[^5] |

- On a 2025 Wrangler 4xe this warranty covers the Auxiliary Hybrid Control Processor, the Battery Pack Control Module, the Belt Driven Hybrid Starter Generator, the Integrated DC/DC Charging Module, the PHEV Brake System Module, the Hybrid Control Processor, the Powertrain Control Module, the Transmission Control Module and the Electronic Brake Boost Control Module.[^5]
- On a 2021 or 2022 Wrangler 4xe it covers the Anti-Lock Brake System Module, the Auxiliary Hybrid Control Processor, the Battery Pack Control Module, the Brake Booster Module, the Hybrid Control Processor, the On-Board Charging Module, the Powertrain Control Module and the Transmission Control Module.[^1][^2]

## Scope note

US market, model years 2021 through 2025, from the Mopar warranty booklet published for each of those years. The 2021, 2022 and 2023 booklets are Wrangler-specific or Wrangler-and-4xe editions; the 2024 booklet is the one Mopar serves under the Wrangler folder, and the 2025 booklet is Jeep's single-brand edition covering gas and PHEV vehicles alike. A 2026 booklet could not be reached on Mopar's publication host, so model year 2026 carries no row here; the booklet delivered with a specific truck is the authority for that truck.
