# Kia EV9: Heat Pump by Model Year and Trim

## Heat pump by model year and trim

| Model year | Trim | Heat pump |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Light, Light Long Range | Not available[^1] |
| 2024 | Wind, Land, GT-Line | Standard[^1] |
| 2025 | Light | Not available[^2] |
| 2025 | Light Long Range | Optional — check the car[^2] |
| 2025 | Wind, Land, GT-Line | Standard[^2] |
| 2026 | Light, Light Long Range | Not available[^3] |
| 2026 | Wind, Land, GT-Line | Standard[^3] |

## How to check an individual car

- Kia puts the air conditioning refrigerant label inside the motor compartment.[^4]
- Item 2 on that label is the amount of refrigerant.[^4]
- With a heat pump the refrigerant charge is 1,300 ± 25 g of R-1234yf; without one, 1,125 ± 25 g.[^5][^6][^7]
- Kia's caveat: the actual label in the vehicle may differ from the illustration.[^4]

## Scope note

Model years 2024 through 2026, US market, each read from Kia America's own Features & Options table for that model year. Kia marks the heat pump Optional on the 2025 Light Long Range and Not Available on the same trim for 2024 and 2026, so a 2025 Light Long Range has to be checked car by car.
