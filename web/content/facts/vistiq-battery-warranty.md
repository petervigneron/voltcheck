# Cadillac Vistiq: Battery Warranty

## Coverage at a glance

Model year 2026:

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The propulsion battery pack and its internal components[^1] |
| Term | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] |
| Capacity floor | 75% of its original value; the battery is replaced or repaired if capacity falls below it during the warranty period, as determined by a certified dealer[^1] |
| Replacement battery | One appropriate for the age and mileage of the vehicle[^1] |
| Repair parts | New or refurbished components, at Cadillac's discretion, for a propulsion battery that needs service due to a defect in materials or workmanship[^1] |
| Where repairs are done | An authorized Cadillac dealership[^1] |
| Cost to the owner | Towing, parts and labor at no charge[^1] |
| Transfers to a later owner | Yes. The warranty is provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period[^1] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is first delivered or put in use[^1] |
| Vehicles it applies to | Cadillac vehicles originally sold, registered, and normally operated in the United States[^1] |
| Bumper-to-bumper coverage | 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] |

## Using the pack for something other than driving

| Covered | Not covered |
|---|---|
| Normal use for backup power during outages, reasonably selling energy back to the electrical grid, and reasonable use as a mobile power source (job site power or vehicle to vehicle charging)[^1] | Excessive use for non-propulsion purposes, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions; General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use that is determined to be harmful to the battery[^2] |

## What is not covered

The booklet's exclusions include:

- Damage to the vehicle's battery from incompatible charging methods, or from an adapter that is not sold or approved by General Motors.[^2]
- Damage resulting from failure to install Over-the-Air software updates; the owner is responsible for ensuring all Cadillac provided Over-the-Air software updates are installed within 45 days of software availability to the vehicle.[^2]
- Alteration, modification, or tampering to the vehicle, including the body, chassis, driveline, software, or other components after final assembly.[^2]
- Non-GM parts, control module calibrations, software modifications, and other alterations not issued through General Motors: they void the coverage for the components they damage or otherwise affect.[^2]
- Damage from aftermarket vehicle propulsion enhancement products or modifications, which may not be covered.[^2]
- Damage caused by collision, fire, theft, freezing, vandalism, riot, explosion, or objects striking the vehicle.[^2]
- Water or fluid contamination.[^2]
- Damage caused by failure to follow the recommended maintenance schedule intervals and/or failure to use or maintain proper fluids, lubricants, or refrigerants between maintenance intervals recommended in the Owner's Manual.[^2]
- Vehicles where the odometer has been disconnected, its reading altered, or mileage cannot be determined.[^2]
- Vehicles currently or previously titled as salvaged, scrapped, junked, or otherwise considered a total loss: the warranty is void.[^2]

## California Zero-Emissions Vehicle (ZEV) warranty coverage

Model year 2026:

| Coverage | Term | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| High voltage battery | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^3] | Defects in materials and workmanship which cause the battery state of health to deteriorate to less than 70%; warranted to the ultimate purchaser and each subsequent purchaser[^3] |
| Propulsion related part, long-term defects | 7 years or 70,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^3] | The parts the booklet marks with an asterisk, among them the Hybrid/Electric Vehicle Battery Pack, Battery Energy Control Module, High Voltage Cables, Front Drive Unit, Rear Drive Unit, Drive Motor Power Inverter and Battery Charger (IPE — OBCM, APM)[^3] |
| Propulsion related part, short-term defects | 3 years or 50,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^3] | Any propulsion-related part[^3] |

The coverage applies only if the vehicle is certified for sale in California (see its emission control information label) and is serviced by an authorized GM dealership in California or in another state that has adopted California's emission warranty requirements. The booklet names Massachusetts, New York, Oregon, Vermont, and Washington.[^3]
