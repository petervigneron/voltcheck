# BMW i4: Battery Warranty

## High-voltage battery limited warranty

| Model year | i4 versions in the booklet | Term |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | i4 eDrive40, i4 M50[^1][^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (12/2021 printing)[^1] |
| 2023 | i4 eDrive40 RWD, i4 M50 AWD, i4 eDrive35 RWD[^2][^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (BMW's 2024 reissue of the 2023 booklet)[^2] |
| 2024 | i4 eDrive40 (AWD), i4 eDrive40 (RWD), i4 M50 (AWD), i4 eDrive35 (RWD)[^3][^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first, unless other available coverage is noted and applicable (06/2024 edition)[^3] |
| 2025 | i4 eDrive35, i4 eDrive40, i4 xDrive40, i4 M50 AWD[^4][^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first, unless other available coverage is noted and applicable (03/2025 edition)[^4] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered, 2022 | The high-voltage lithium-ion battery assembly, against defects in materials or workmanship[^1] |
| What is covered, 2023 | The high-voltage lithium-ion (traction) battery assembly, against defects in materials or workmanship[^2] |
| What is covered, 2024–2025 | The high-voltage (traction) battery assembly, against defects in materials or workmanship[^3][^4] |
| Repair standard | Repair or replacement of the defective part(s) with new or authorized remanufactured parts, without charge for parts and labor (including diagnosis), at BMW NA's sole choice[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Transfers to a second owner | Yes. The battery warranty is part of the New Vehicle Limited Warranty, which BMW NA gives on U.S.-specification vehicles distributed by BMW NA or sold through the BMW NA European Delivery Program, to the first retail purchaser and each subsequent purchaser[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Clock starts | The date of first retail sale or the date the vehicle is first placed into service as a sales demonstrator, Aftersales Mobility Program (AMP) Vehicle or company vehicle, whichever is earlier[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Where it is valid | U.S.-specification vehicles, at an authorized BMW center in the United States, including Puerto Rico[^1][^2][^3][^4] |

## What is not covered

Among the items the New Vehicle Limited Warranty lists under "What is not covered" in the 2022–2025 booklets:

- 2022: Damage, including consequential, which results from negligence, misuse/improper operation of the vehicle, improper repair, lack of or improper maintenance, environmental influences, flood, accident or fire damage, road salt corrosion, described in the Owner's Manual.[^1]
- 2023–2025: Damage, including consequential, which results from negligence, misuse/improper operation of the vehicle, improper repair, lack of or improper maintenance, environmental influences, flood, accident or fire damage, road salt corrosion, or the use of improper fuel as described in the Owner's Manual or contaminated fuel.[^2][^3][^4]
- Modification of the vehicle or installation of any performance accessories or components attached to the vehicle which alters the original engineering and/or operating specifications or which results in damage to the other original components, electrical interference, electrical short, radio static, water leaks and wind noise.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- Non-genuine BMW parts: BMW NA is not obligated to pay for repairs of the non-genuine BMW parts or for repairs of any damage resulting from the use of non-genuine parts.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- The warranty is null and void for specific vehicle components that were previously replaced with used or salvaged automobile parts, including repairs of any damage resulting from the use of these parts.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- The warranty is null and void if the odometer has been replaced or altered and the true mileage on the vehicle cannot be determined, and/or the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is altered and/or cannot be determined.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- The warranty is null and void if the vehicle has been declared a total loss or sold for salvage purposes, or if the vehicle has been used in any competitive event.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
