# Ford Mustang Mach-E (model years 2021–2025): Heat Pump by Year

## 2021–2024

No Mustang Mach-E built for model years 2021 through 2024 has a heat pump, on any trim or battery pack.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

## 2025

Every 2025 Mustang Mach-E has a Vapor Injection Heat Pump. It is standard equipment on Select,[^5] and Premium and GT each carry all Select equipment plus their own additions.[^6]

Est. Before model year 2025, the Mach-E used resistance heating instead of a heat pump.[^7]

## 2026

Not covered. No primary document for model year 2026 was opened for this sheet.
