# Ford Mustang Mach-E: Heat Pump by Model Year

## Heat pump by model year

| Model year | Heat pump |
|---|---|
| 2021 | None[^1] |
| 2022 | None[^2] |
| 2023 | None[^3] |
| 2024 | None[^4] |
| 2025 | Standard, all trims — Vapor Injection Heat Pump[^5][^6] |

- Est. Before model year 2025, the Mach-E used resistance heating instead of a heat pump.[^7]
