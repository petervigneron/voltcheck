# BMW i7: Battery Warranty

## High-voltage battery limited warranty, all states

| Model year | Term |
|---|---|
| 2023[^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (BMW's 2024 reissue of the 2023 booklet)[^1] |
| 2024[^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (June 2024 edition)[^2] |
| 2025[^5] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (March 2025 edition)[^3] |
| 2026[^5] | 96 months (8 years) or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first (September 2025 edition)[^4] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered, 2023–2025 | The high-voltage (traction) battery assembly, against defects in materials or workmanship[^1][^2][^3] |
| What is covered, 2026 | The high-voltage traction battery assembly and its internal components, against defects in materials and/or workmanship[^4] |
| Capacity coverage, 2026 | Usable (net) capacity/State of Health below 70 percent, as confirmed by an authorized BMW center, for 96 months (8 years) or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first; BMW restores the battery's capacity to at least 70 percent[^4] |
| Repair standard, 2023–2025 | Repair or replacement of the defective part(s) with new or authorized remanufactured parts, at BMW NA's sole choice[^1][^2][^3] |
| Repair standard, 2026 | A software update, or repair and/or replacement of the appropriate HV battery components with new, refurbished and/or reconditioned parts, at BMW NA's sole choice; the minimum net HV battery capacity after repair is at least 70 percent[^4] |
| Transfers to a second owner, 2023–2025 | Yes. The battery warranty is part of the New Vehicle Limited Warranty, which BMW NA gives on U.S.-specification vehicles distributed by BMW NA or sold through the BMW NA European Delivery Program, to the first retail purchaser and each subsequent purchaser[^1][^2][^3] |
| Clock starts, 2023–2025 | The date of first retail sale or the date the vehicle is first placed into service as a sales demonstrator, Aftersales Mobility Program (AMP) Vehicle or company vehicle, whichever is earlier[^1][^2][^3] |
| Where it is valid | U.S.-specification vehicles, serviced at an authorized BMW center in the United States, including Puerto Rico[^1][^2][^3][^4] |

## What is not covered, 2026

The 2026 high-voltage battery warranty's "What Is Not Covered" section lists:

- Issues caused by not adhering to the Owner's Manual instructions regarding the high-voltage battery (especially instructions about its service life), or issues/damage caused by not charging the BEV model within 14 days after the charge level indicator shows "0 % / 0 km (mile)" range remaining.[^4]
- Issues/damage caused by using non-BMW approved third-party charging equipment (charging cables, charging plugs) and/or defective/damaged charging infrastructure equipment, such as public charging stations.[^4]
- HV battery assembly physical damage caused by an external heat source, fire/open flame, vandalism, accident, storm, flooding and/or lightning strike, and damage due to an act of God.[^4]
- Immersion of the HV battery assembly and/or parts of it in water or other liquids, including direct exposure to high-pressure cleaning devices (except automated car washes).[^4]
- Exposing the HV battery to a corrosive environment, for example a highly acidic or highly alkaline environment and/or cleaning agents.[^4]
- Non-compliance with the BEV model's load limits and/or towing capacities, when applicable, as specified by BMW.[^4]
- Use and operation of the BEV model as an off-board power source for functions other than driving the vehicle, its factory-installed equipment and/or BMW approved accessories.[^4]
- Use and operation of the HV battery after its removal from the vehicle.[^4]
- Non-genuine BMW parts: BMW NA is not obligated to pay for repairs of the non-genuine BMW parts or for repairs of any damage resulting from the use of non-genuine parts.[^4]
- The warranty is null and void for specific vehicle components that were previously replaced with used or salvaged automobile parts, including repairs of any damage resulting from the use of these parts.[^4]
- The warranty is null and void if the odometer has been replaced or altered and the true mileage cannot be determined, and/or the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is altered and/or cannot be determined.[^4]
- The warranty is null and void if the vehicle has been declared a total loss or sold for salvage purposes.[^4]
- The warranty is null and void if the vehicle has been used in any competitive event activities (for example, overstraining the HV battery).[^4]

## What voids it, 2023–2025

- The warranty is null and void for specific vehicle components that were previously replaced with used or salvaged automobile parts, including repairs of any damage resulting from the use of these parts.[^1][^2][^3]
- The warranty is null and void if the odometer has been replaced or altered and the true mileage on the vehicle cannot be determined, and/or the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is altered and/or cannot be determined.[^1][^2][^3]
- The warranty is null and void if the vehicle has been declared a total loss or sold for salvage purposes, or if the vehicle has been used in any competitive event.[^1][^2][^3]
