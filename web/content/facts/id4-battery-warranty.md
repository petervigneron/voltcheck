# Volkswagen ID.4: Battery Warranty

## High-voltage battery coverage

| Model year | Term |
|---|---|
| 2021 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^1] |
| 2022 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^2] |
| 2023 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^3] |
| 2024 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^4] |
| 2025 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^5] |
| 2026 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first[^6] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Warranty name | High-Voltage System Limited Warranty, All-electric models only[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| What the battery term covers | Defects in material and workmanship, and net capacity loss below 70% (2021–2025) or State of Health loss below 70% (2026)[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Capacity floor | 70% net capacity (2021–2025) or 70% State of Health (2026); a reduction of up to 30% is not a defect under the warranty[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Repair standard | Repairs return the battery to 70% net capacity (2021–2025) or 70% State of Health (2026); a complete replacement battery provides at least that 70%; Volkswagen does not commit to an "as new" 100%[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Parts used | New or remanufactured Genuine Volkswagen Parts, free of charge[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Rest of the high-voltage system | 4 years or 50,000 miles: power electronics (DC-to-DC converter and pulse inverter), electric motor, high-voltage air conditioning, high-voltage heating, charger, charging socket, and orange high-voltage cables and connectors[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Transfers to a second owner | Yes, automatically and without cost, when ownership changes within the warranty period[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Clock starts | The original in-service date: delivery to the original purchaser or original lessee, or the date the vehicle was first placed in service as a "demonstrator" or "company" car[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |
| Who services it | A certified Volkswagen High-Voltage Specialist[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |

## What is not covered

Volkswagen's 2021 through 2026 booklets exclude:

- Vehicles used for commercial purposes, including taxi, courier or delivery service, or limousine, including after sale to a later retail owner.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- A battery defect caused by the battery not being used, handled or maintained as described in the owner's manual.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Damage or malfunctions caused by improper charging of the High-Voltage System.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Damage or malfunctions caused by operating the vehicle in areas flooded with water higher than the bottom of the vehicle body.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Damage or malfunctions caused by high-pressure water, steam jet cleaners, or harsh cleaning chemicals applied directly to the high-voltage battery.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Damage from accident, collision, vandalism, fire or competitive events, or from continued operation after a warning light or gauge indicates a problem.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Vehicles severely damaged or declared a total loss by an insurer, or substantially re-assembled from or repaired with salvage parts.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Replacement of High-Voltage System components that wear from normal use or deterioration.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Damage from failure to follow the recommended maintenance; the dealer will deny coverage without service or repair orders proving it was done.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
- Incidental or consequential damage, including loss of value of the vehicle.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

## Scope note

US market, model years 2021 through 2026, from Volkswagen's USA Warranty and Maintenance booklet for each model year. The 2026 booklet states the battery's 70% coverage as State of Health where the 2021–2025 booklets say net capacity. The booklet delivered with a specific car is the authority for that car.
