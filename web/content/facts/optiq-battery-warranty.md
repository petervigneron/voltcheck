# Cadillac Optiq: Battery Warranty

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is covered | The propulsion battery pack and its internal components[^1][^2] |
| Term | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^2][^3] |
| Capacity floor | The battery is replaced or repaired if capacity falls below 75% of its original value during the warranty period, as determined by a certified dealer[^1][^2] |
| Replacement standard | A battery appropriate for the age and mileage of the vehicle; Cadillac repairs or replaces the propulsion battery with new or refurbished components at its discretion[^1][^2] |
| Who does the work | Cadillac's network of certified dealers trained to perform repairs on electric vehicle battery packs[^1][^2] |
| Transfers to a second owner | Yes. The warranty is provided to the original owner and any subsequent owners of the vehicle during the warranty period[^1][^2] |
| Clock starts | The date the vehicle is first delivered or put in use[^1][^2] |
| Where it applies | Cadillac vehicles originally sold, registered, and normally operated in the United States[^1][^2] |
| Rest of the vehicle | Bumper-to-bumper, 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1][^2][^3] |
| Powering a house or the grid | Normal use of the main battery for backup power during outages, for reasonably selling energy back to the electrical grid, or for reasonable use as a mobile power source is covered. Additional vehicle and in-home hardware may be required[^1][^2] |

## California Zero-Emissions Vehicle coverage (2026)

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| High Voltage Battery Warranty | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^4] |
| What it guarantees | The battery is free from defects in materials and workmanship which cause the battery state of health to deteriorate to less than 70%[^5] |
| Propulsion-related part short-term defects | 3 years or 50,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^4][^5] |
| Propulsion-related part long-term defects | 7 years or 70,000 miles, whichever first occurs[^4][^5] |
| Who qualifies | Both must be true: the vehicle is certified for sale in California, per its emission control information label; and it is serviced by an authorized GMC or GM dealership located in California or in another state that has specifically adopted California's emission warranty requirements[^6] |
| Those other states | Colorado, Delaware, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Vermont, Washington, and Washington D.C.[^6] |
| If either condition fails | The California Zero-Emissions Vehicle coverage above does not apply[^6] |

## What is not covered

Cadillac excludes from the New Vehicle Limited Warranty:

- Damage to the battery from incompatible charging methods, or from an adapter that is not sold or approved by General Motors.[^7]
- Damage resulting from failure to install Cadillac-provided over-the-air software updates within 45 days of the software becoming available to the vehicle.[^7]
- Excessive use of the main battery for non-propulsion purposes, including semi-permanent or permanent daily power provisions. General Motors reserves the right to curtail excessive non-propulsion use it determines to be harmful to the battery.[^8]
- Damage caused by collision, fire, theft, freezing, vandalism, riot, explosion, or objects striking the vehicle; and misuse such as spinning the wheels, driving over curbs, overloading, racing or other competition.[^8]
- Alteration, modification, or tampering with the vehicle after final assembly, including its software; installation of non-GM parts; and water or fluid contamination.[^9]
- Damage caused by failure to follow the recommended maintenance schedule intervals, or by failure to use or maintain proper fluids, lubricants, or refrigerants.[^8]
- Vehicles currently or previously titled as salvaged, scrapped, junked, or otherwise considered a total loss: the warranty is void on those.[^9]

## Scope note

US Cadillac Optiq, model years 2025 and 2026, from the Cadillac Electric Vehicle warranty booklet for each of those years. The California Zero-Emissions Vehicle section exists only in the 2026 edition, which is why that table is marked 2026. The Optiq-V is a separate model and nothing above is a claim about it; model year 2027 is not covered here, and the booklet delivered with a specific car is the authority for that car.
