- Charging slows down as the battery recharges. GM's advice: charge to 80% for daily driving or when driving in mountainous terrain, and above 80% for long trips when the route is not mountainous.[^8]
- Fast Charge Prep, if equipped, brings the battery to its optimal temperature for quicker DC charging. Adding a DC charge station to a Google Maps route starts it automatically. Started from the infotainment screen instead, begin it about 30 minutes before charging.[^9]

## Connector and adapters

| Model year | Charge port |
|---|---|
| 2024 | Combined Charging System 1 (CCS1)[^6] |
| 2025 | Combined Charging System 1 (CCS1)[^7] |
| 2026 | Combined Charging System 1 (CCS1)[^8][^11] |
| 2027 | North American Charging Standard (NACS)[^5][^10][^11] |

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tesla Supercharger access, CCS1 cars | Requires a GM-approved NACS DC adapter plus public charging set up in the myChevrolet app; order it on Chevrolet Accessories, in the app, or from a dealer[^4][^12] |
| NACS DC adapter price and warranty | $225 MSRP, 12-month limited warranty from the date of purchase[^12] |
| Superchargers reachable with it | Select generation 3 (V3) and beyond — initially over 17,800 across North America[^12] |
| The adapter at Tesla Level 2 chargers | Not compatible — it is a DC adapter for Tesla Superchargers only[^12] |
| 2027 cars at a CCS station | A NACS-to-CCS adapter is required[^10][^11] |
| Third-party adapters | Only GM-approved adapters. Damage caused by an adapter that GM does not sell, provide or approve is not covered by the vehicle warranty[^8][^10][^13] |
| Charge port door | Powered opening, if equipped; push and release the top left corner. With 12-volt power lost, do not open it manually — it opens once the 12-volt battery is recharged or jumped. The charge port does not charge the 12-volt battery[^8][^10] |
