# Ford F-150 Lightning: Battery Warranty

## High voltage battery coverage

| Model year | Electric Vehicle Component coverage | High Voltage Lithium-Ion Battery Capacity Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^1] |
| 2023 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^2] |
| 2024 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^3] |
| 2025 | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^4] | 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first[^4] |

## Coverage at a glance

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Components covered | High voltage battery assembly, Bussed Electrical Center (BEC), Battery Energy Control Module (BECM), on-board charger, Inverter System Controller (ISC), DC/DC converter, and eDrive[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Capacity floor | Capacity below 70 percent of the high voltage battery's beginning-of-life capacity, as determined by an authorized EV Certified Ford Dealer[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Capacity measurement | Ford's method, at Ford's sole discretion[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Replacement parts | New, factory remanufactured, or factory refurbished, at Ford's discretion; refurbished battery components align with the vehicle's age and mileage[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Where the battery is repaired | An EV Certified Ford dealership[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Transfers to a later owner | Yes. A buyer of a previously owned vehicle of that model year is eligible for any remaining warranty coverages[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Clock starts | The Warranty Start Date: the day the new vehicle is delivered or the day it is first put into service, for example as a dealer demonstrator, whichever occurs first[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Clock starts, California: 2023 guide (March 2025 Fifth Printing), 2024 and 2025 guides | For new retail sales on or after July 1, 2023 sold from a California retailer and/or to a California resident, the Warranty Start Date and mileage start no earlier than delivery to the customer[^2][^3][^4] |
| California or other emissions warranty | None. Fully-electric vehicles are not eligible for any emissions warranties[^1][^2][^3][^4] |
| Powertrain Coverage | 5 years or 60,000 miles, whichever occurs first, for driveline components not covered by the Electric Vehicle Component warranty[^1][^2][^3][^4] |

- The capacity coverage is in addition to the high voltage battery assembly's limited warranty for defects in materials or workmanship.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

## What is not covered

Ford's 2022 through 2025 booklets treat gradual capacity loss with time and use as normal wear and tear, and exclude damage caused by:[^1][^2][^3][^4]

- Accidents, collision or objects striking the vehicle (including driving through a car wash).
- Fire or explosion.
- Immersion or flooding of the high voltage battery assembly.
- Abuse or misuse of the vehicle or the high voltage battery pack, such as driving over curbs, overloading, racing, or using the vehicle as a permanent stationary power source.
- Failing to observe and resolve vehicle warnings and indications of battery concerns within "a reasonable amount of time, typically 30 days."
- Failing to complete Ford Power-Up software updates within "a reasonable amount of time, typically 30 days."
- Using incompatible charging devices or methods.
- Modifications to the high voltage battery assembly, the high voltage system, or associated wiring.
- Exposure to direct or intense heat sources outside of normal use, such as collision repairs that subject the vehicle to paint booth conditions that exceed Ford's recommendations.
- Leaving the high voltage battery assembly fully discharged, or at a near zero state of charge, for more than 14 days.
- Repairs performed by facilities and personnel not authorized by Ford.
- Improper vehicle storage resulting in high voltage battery damage.

## Scope note

US market, model years 2022 through 2025, from the Battery Electric Vehicle Warranty Guide Ford publishes for each model year, which covers the E-Transit, Mustang Mach-E and F-150 Lightning together. The battery terms, the components covered and the exclusions read the same in all four booklets; only the California start-date note is absent from the 2022 booklet. The booklet delivered with a specific truck is the authority for that truck.
