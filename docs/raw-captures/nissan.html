      TAKATA RECALL

      Takata airbag safety recall information

      Certain Nissan vehicles may have a defective airbag which needs to be repaired immediately. In the event of a crash, the airbag could explode, shooting metal pieces at the driver and passengers, causing serious injury or death. If your vehicle is affected by this recall, we encourage you to take immediate action to have your vehicles repaired for free at an authorized Nissan dealer. 

      Nissan is issuing an immediate DO NOT DRIVE warning for certain model year 2002-2006 Nissan Sentra and 2002-2004 Nissan Pathfinder vehicles in the United States and Canada affected by open Takata airbag recalls. Nissan strongly advises drivers not to drive affected vehicles until the FREE repair has been completed.

      RECALL & SERVICE INFORMATION

      NHTSA safety recalls & service information

      A vehicle recall is issued when a manufacturer or the department of National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) determines a vehicle (or a piece of equipment inside) creates a safety risk, or fails to meet minimum safety standards. Manufacturers are required to fix the problem by offering repairs, replacement or a refund to owners of the problem vehicle.

      Nissan is committed to the safety and security of our customers and their passengers. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience the recall may cause you. This page will be periodically updated with new information, so please refer back.

      Still have questions about recalls?

      The answers to your most frequently asked questions are right here.

      How do I know if my Nissan is affected by a recall?

      Using your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the the lookup tool, you can check if any recalls apply to your Nissan. 

      Will Nissan provide a loaner car if my vehicle is affected by a recall?

      Yes. A free loaner car, towing service, or mobile repair in select locations may be available to assist with any inconveniences resulting from the recall. 

      Which Nissan models are being recalled?

      Please use the Nissan Recall Lookup tool to confirm whether your vehicle is affected and to obtain vehicle-specific instructions.

      What is a VIN and where can I find the VIN number on my vehicle?

      A Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) acts as an identifier for Nissan vehicles. The VIN can be found on the driver's side of the dashboard, door post, or vehicle registration.

      Where can I find my VIN?

      Where can I find my vehicle identification number (VIN)?

      Your Nissan’s dashboard

      You’ll find your VIN on the driver’s side of your vehicle, where the windshield meets the dashboard.

      Front door post

      A sticker on the driver’s side door post displays your VIN.

      Proof-of-insurance card

      You can find your VIN on your vehicle’s certificate of insurance.

      Vehicle registration

      Your VIN also appears on your vehicle registration card.

      Frequently asked questions

      Frequently asked questions

      Is this a safety recall?

      Yes

      What is the reason for safety recall?

      Due to a supplier issue that has since been corrected, the ABS actuator pump seal in some vehicles may leak brake fluid, causing the electric circuit board to malfunction. If this occurs, the ABS warning lamp will illuminate and remain continuously illuminated on the instrument panel. However, if the vehicle continues to be operated with this condition, the leak could potentially create an electric short, which in rare instances, could result in a fire.

      What is the correction plan for this voluntary safety recall campaign?

      The dealer will inspect the serial number of the ABS actuator and, if affected, replace the actuator with a new one free of charge. If your vehicle was inspected as part of a previous recall (16V-636), your ABS actuator will be replaced with a new one, at no cost you.

      What model year vehicles are affected?

      Certain MY2016-2017 Nissan Maxima, MY2015-2017 Nissan Murano and MY2017-2018 Nissan Pathfinder vehicles.

      Are any INFINITI models affected by this recall?

      Yes. Certain MY2017 INFINITI QX60 vehicles are also affected. Please visit http://www.infinitiusa.com/recalls-vin/ for more information about INFINITI models.

      How can I know if my vehicle is affected?

      You may enter your VIN to see if your vehicle is affected by visiting the Nissan VIN look up website at http://www.nissanusa.com/recalls-vin/

      When will parts be available to repair my vehicle?

      Parts are available. Contact your Nissan dealer to schedule a vehicle inspection and repair.

      I previously paid to have my ABS actuator replaced. Can I be reimbursed for the repair?

      If you have paid to have your ABS actuator replaced due to the warning lamp being ON, you may be eligible for reimbursement of the related expense. 

      Will a rental vehicle be provided while the dealer is servicing my vehicle?

      If, after inspection, your vehicle requires the ABS actuator to be replaced, a rental will be provided until your vehicle has been repaired.

      Is my vehicle safe to drive until it is repaired?

      If the ABS Warning Light is ON, do not drive your vehicle; park it outside away from structures and other vehicles. Have your vehicle towed to a Nissan dealer by calling Nissan Roadside Assistance at 1-800-225-2476.


      If the ABS Warning Light is Not ON, owners may drive their vehicle at their own discretion, but Nissan urges owners to bring their vehicle to a Nissan dealer as soon as possible to have their ABS actuator inspected.

      Is there anything owners can do to mitigate this risk?

      Bring the vehicle to the dealer for inspection as soon as possible if the vehicle is subject to this recall. If the ABS warning lamp continuously illuminates after starting the vehicle, stop driving the vehicle immediately. Park it outside and away from structures and other vehicles. Have your vehicle towed to a Nissan dealer by calling Nissan Roadside Assistance at 1-800-225-2476.

      Will I be charged for the repair?

      No, the repair will be performed for the customer free of charge for parts and labor.

      Will I have to take my vehicle back to the selling dealer to have the service performed?

      No, any authorized Nissan dealer is able to perform the recall campaign.
